Collateral and risk management

November 25: KuCoin sets out shared insurance funds and two-stage auto-deleveraging

Starting December 1, KuCoin plans to assign pairs to individual or shared insurance funds according to risk. If a shared pool comes under stress, symbol-level auto-deleveraging will first target the contracts contributing the most to losses. It will extend to the entire pool only if conditions worsen. KuCoin also plans an API for querying balances by pool, pair or settlement currency. Trigger thresholds are subject to the rules in effect at launch.

November 25: Binance adds five bStocks to spot trading and margin collateral

Binance listed ARTYB, FRHCB, IBITB, IGVB and SOXXB for spot trading and made them eligible collateral for cross margin, portfolio margin and Portfolio Margin Pro. November 24: Researchers test GPT-6.2 protein designs against five targets

OpenAI reported that researchers tested 322 small binders designed with GPT-6.2 against five targets. Of those, 35 showed binding. Antibody fragments revised by the model also retained binding activity. GPT-6.2 used reasoning and code for design, but candidate screening still relied on specialist structure models. Its full protein-folding predictions lagged behind, limiting the scope of the result.
