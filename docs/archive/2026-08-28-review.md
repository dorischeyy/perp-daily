# Editorial review
Date: 2026-08-28 · Issue 61

## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: KuCoin introduced a unified contract API with distinct premarket, open, settlement and closed states. Bitrue documented how remaining positions are handled in a hard delisting. Exchanges are making the market lifecycle available to trading systems through their interfaces.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: Alongside authentication changes, UTA V2 adds a premarket-to-perpetual transition date, settlement time, funding, maximum market order size and distinct market states. Interfaces, order handling and risk controls can use the same definitions.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: From considering suspension, removal, and settlement as front-end operational configurations, it has been updated to consider the lifecycle as the primary machine state for transaction integration.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: In product development, it is necessary to verify whether the market state is consistent across the front end, orders, Keeper, and configurations before deciding whether to expand the pool or adjust the exit process. Investment research should not confuse equity perpetual futures with mirror subscriptions and underlying stock rights.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If the detailed states of UTA V2 are not utilized by the listing party, or if there are still frequent errors in switching orders, the value of the interface should be downgraded. If the on-chain architecture cannot reflect these states, then the data cannot be directly migrated.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: All candidates that only feature new currency names, single asset specifications, or company promotions, without any mechanisms or boundaries, have been removed. All selected entries have been supplemented with details regarding rights, levels, or implementation restrictions.
