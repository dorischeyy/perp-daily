1. **Why open this edition today?**: The same market snapshot allows for direct comparison of total positions, turnover, and market coverage among Hyperliquid, Bybit, Gate, and MEXC. This reveals differences in risk density behind different market sizes.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: Hyperliquid’s total OI is almost the same as Bybit’s. However, Hyperliquid’s OI is 2.4 times higher than Bybit’s daily transactions. Focusing solely on positions or transactions would lead to missing out on potential risks.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: The method of determining the scale of venues was changed from using total OI and trading volume to focusing on the rolling ratio of OI to executable transactions, thereby determining risk capacity.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: It was prioritized to include the trend of position turnover in the market parameters. Leverage or OI limits were tightened only when the situation continued to deteriorate and depth also decreased.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If asset-level data shows a high OI-to-transaction ratio over multiple days, but this is not accompanied by an increase in price differences, a decrease in depth, or an increase in liquidation losses, then the risk control priority of this indicator should be lowered.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: Ordinary transaction pairs were removed from the new listing. There were no subsequent activity lists, and only AI updates with model slogans were retained. All entries clearly stated the mechanisms or verification boundaries.
