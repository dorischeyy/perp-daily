# Editorial review

## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: Trading has resumed, but OI growth is almost synchronous. 86% of the incremental OI from the three major venues comes from Gate. Kraken and Kalshi have transformed trading capital and low-latency data into new distribution layers for RWA and prediction markets.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: Gate’s OI increased by 33.8% in a single day, while trading volume decreased by 27.8%. This suggests that the total capacity may be determined by the capacity of a single venue.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: RWA perpetual competition now involves not only comparing targets, trading hours, and settled assets, but also determining who provides trading capital, filtering mechanisms, and institutional data access.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating new RWA markets, it’s important to check the sources of margin, initial liquidity, ongoing transactions, and data consumers. The decision should not be based solely on the number of feeds.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If the OI, account distribution, and order depth of Gate’s trading pairs can explain the increase, or if Kraken-funded accounts do not actually execute trades, then the weights of data anomalies and capital distribution should be reduced accordingly.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: Ordinary currency prices, ETF performance, rumors about financing without actual data, as well as AI watermarks and duplicate information related to Gemini monthly usage were removed.
