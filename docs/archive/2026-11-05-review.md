# Perp DEX Daily editorial review

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Competition in traditional-asset perpetuals now extends to fee subsidies and product design. Lighter has also made matching, liquidations and emergency exits verifiable, adding evidence on available products, execution and exit risk.

**What is the most important finding?**: Binance has reduced its high-tier VIP discounts, indicating that the platform is starting to use sustained fees to compete for TradFi order flows. However, low fees, daily trading, and multi-product models still cannot replace price sources, depth, and exit mechanisms.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: Traditional-asset perpetuals now compete on fee subsidies, continuous and dated contracts, tokenized spot assets and verifiable execution, alongside market coverage and regulated access.

**What decision can readers make?**: Evaluate fees, product rights, price sources, depth outside cash-market hours, the scope of verification, and emergency exits separately. A launch announcement or an onchain settlement claim covers only part of that assessment.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If low fees do not lead to sustained transactions and OI, or if verification shows that execution cannot be completed in time under extreme load conditions, or if priority requests and Escape Hatch cannot actually complete exit procedures, then it is necessary to lower the fees and re-evaluate the product’s value.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Ordinary expansion items, duplicate items, and candidates that only serve for model promotion but do not change product, transaction, or risk judgments have been deleted.

### Six reader perspectives

- Products: Separate the rights differences of continuous perials, expiration-type X-Perp, and tokenized physical assets. Do not consider similar targets as the same product.
- Transactions: Low fees and 24-hour order placement do not equal the same depth of trading during market closures. The text retains the price source and liquidity gap.
- Risk: Describe Binance’s backfill, its reserve snapshot and its complete zero-knowledge proof separately, so that progress is not presented as a final, independently verifiable result.
- Newcomers: Lighter only explains what constraints can be proven, what cannot be proven, and how to exit when the sorter fails.
- Operations: There is no deadline for fee discounts; they are not considered permanent rates or stable order flows.
- Engineering: AI retains permissions, governance, false positives, or effectiveness data. Product release is not considered a production validation.
