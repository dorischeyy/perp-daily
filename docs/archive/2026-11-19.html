Perpetuals funding and new traditional-asset markets

November 19: Bybit announces funding rates scaled to each perpetual’s actual settlement interval

Starting November 24, Bybit will update its perpetual funding formula in three batches. It will multiply the eight-hour base rate by N/8, where N is the actual interval in hours, and then apply the rate caps. Estimated rates and payments may change for contracts with intervals shorter than eight hours. Positions, default intervals and previously settled fees are unaffected. The final batch is scheduled for December 1.

November 18: Binance and OKX set different funding rules for their SOXX perpetuals

Binance added round-the-clock TradFi perpetuals on SOXX, IGV, ARTY, IBIT, CCJ and ETN. OKX listed SOXX, XLF and the leveraged ETF SSPC. Both venues settle in USDT. If funding reaches its ceiling or floor, Binance keeps eight-hour settlement while OKX automatically switches to hourly settlement. That difference changes how a SOXX position accrues funding costs.

November 18: Kalshi begins a rolling listing of 36 new single-stock perpetuals

Kalshi certified 36 new single-stock perpetuals with the CFTC, covering 33 common stocks and three ADRs, and planned to list them on a rolling basis from November 18. They use existing approved product terms, cash settlement and daily funding to keep prices aligned with the underlying. Trading hours reflect U.S. cash-market holidays and early closes. Customers access the contracts through intermediaries subject to suitability rules.

November 17: Bullish and Equiniti’s issuer-token consortium grows to 12 members

On November 18th, Meta integrated Loblaw payments into Muse and isolated payment credentials.

Loblaw became Muse’s first Canadian retail partner. Users can plan meals, build a basket and check out within the agent. Meta said that passwords and payment methods stay in the Muse Secure VM, outside the model’s view. Users choose which connections to authorize and must confirm sensitive actions such as purchases. The retail integration pairs checkout with isolated credentials and transaction-level approval.
