# Editorial review

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Perpetual platforms no longer compare only the number of assets. trade.xyz actively reduces the leverage in the financial reporting window. Aster and Ondo focus on strategies for integrating spot markets and maintaining margins. Risk capacity and capital reuse become key competitive factors in product development.

**What is the most important finding?**: trade.xyz reduced the maximum leverage for NVDA to 10 times from 20 times. This indicates that stock perpetual contracts must dynamically adjust their risk capacity based on real-world events such as financial reports. It’s not enough to rely solely on fixed leverage and funding costs.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The original assessment focused on handling expansion, split-up, and pre-market parameter adjustments during the stock perpetual lifecycle. Now, it’s shifted to temporary leverage reduction during financial reporting periods. Additionally, spot assets are used as a strategy and margin for risk management.

**What decision can readers make?**: For products and risk management, it’s advisable to check the event calendar, existing position handling, margin discounts, batch cancellations, and points redemption. No new assets or account mergers are needed to replace the complete risk rules.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If the reduction of leverage before the financial report was not actually implemented, if spot prices cannot truly support perpetual margin requirements, if combined orders cannot be canceled individually, or if no users have migrated to new products, then today’s judgment should be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Excluded routine listings, repeated corporate actions, promotions for 1,000x leveraged equity perpetuals without risk parameters, product roundups lacking official details and old AI updates.
