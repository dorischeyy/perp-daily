**Which prior assessment changed?**: The US perpetuals classification series gained evidence on designated contract market product certification. The frontier model evaluation series expanded from one authorization incident to four verifiable failure categories and layered safeguards.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating perpetual products, it’s important to separate compliance registration, trading openness, and real OI. When designing agents, it’s essential to include permissions, monitoring, blocking, and manual approvals within the system’s procedures.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If CDNA certification does not translate into any tradable products, Castle Labs’ aggregated disclosures may lead to double counting of OI, and Thai regulatory rules change the 80% exposure requirement. Related judgments should be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Deleted pages related to ordinary contracts for listing tokens, those without any new mechanisms, AI news articles that only mention cooperation visions, and entries where daily token price changes are presented as structural trends.
