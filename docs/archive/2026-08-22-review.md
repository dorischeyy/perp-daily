## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: The trading volume in four major markets has rebounded by 27.2%. However, OI continues to decline, indicating that market activity recovery does not equal the return of leverage funds. At the same time, Binance has moved general AI agents to the futures execution layer.

**What is the most important finding?**: What’s most worth noting is Binance’s use of independent sub-accounts, prohibitions on withdrawals, and order confirmation to carry out brokerage transactions. This transforms brokerage risk management from theoretical concepts to a real-world model implemented by leading exchanges.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The approach has changed from allocating independent risk budgets to agents to requiring platform-side account isolation, withdrawal restrictions, and individual confirmation of risks. Risk boundaries cannot rely solely on agents’ compliance.

**What decision can readers make?**: The product team can first implement read-only proxy access. The execution capability must be ready only when independent accounts, whitelists, nominal value hard limits, individual confirmation, revocations, and logs are all in place.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If subsequent data shows that agents can maintain extremely low error rates without isolation from sub-accounts, and they do not suffer any asset losses, and they pass independent security assessments, then the necessity of current forced isolation can be reduced.

**Which news recap was removed?**: The article discussing Bitcoin’s daily increases and short-selling settlements has been deleted, as it only described price fluctuations and did not contribute to understanding the structure of the market, risk mechanisms, or product decisions.
