Robinhood Chain TVL rises more than 160% to $234 million as meme trading grows and RWA perpetuals remain waitlisted

Event: Dune recorded a one-day TVL increase of more than 160% on July 7 to $234 million, helped by about $50 million in Ethena stablecoin deposits. Daily DEX volume peaked near $400 million. Total addresses approached 200,000 and daily active addresses rose from near zero to tens of thousands. The result was still below the $300 million–$1 billion TVL range used here for major perpetual DEXs. This was the eighth update since July 2.

Mechanism: On July 8, pump.fun added Robinhood Chain token trading through a single Solana wallet without a separate bridge step. CASHCAT rose a reported 700–950% in 24 hours, reaching a market capitalization of approximately $68 million–$100 million. Meanwhile, Arcus’s 35 planned real-world asset perpetual markets remained on a waitlist after the July 1 network launch.

Second-order effects: The activity demonstrates demand for meme-token trading. It provides little evidence about demand for the proposed brokerage-distributed perpetual markets while they remain unavailable. If Arcus takes longer to open, the launchpad may capture more immediate value from the chain’s distribution.

Binance Research puts first-half stablecoin-settled perpetual volume on traditional assets above $1.1 trillion

Event: Binance Research reported on July 8 that stablecoin-settled perpetuals linked to traditional assets such as stocks and commodities exceeded $1.1 trillion in volume in the first half of 2026. Their share of all crypto perpetuals volume was about 11% on the report's separate five-month measure.

Mechanism: The report also pointed to longer-term stablecoin holdings. The proportion of Binance users who held more than half of their portfolios in stablecoins rose from 4% in 2020 to 30%. Global stablecoin capitalization was about $311 billion, and June transfers reached a record $1.79 trillion.

Second-order effects: Together, products such as HIP-3 equity perpetuals, Ondo Perps and the traditional-asset contracts at Binance and OKX accounted for an estimated 11% of the broader market on the report's measure. The aggregate provides a better indication of scale than individual launches.

Bitcoin drops to $61,500 as the Iran ceasefire breaks down and market-wide liquidations reach $331 million

On July 8 US Eastern time, President Trump said the ceasefire with Iran had ended and announced plans for further airstrikes after allegations that Iran had attacked vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. Brent rose 6.3% to $78.80 a barrel. BTC fell from $63,600 to $61,500 in 24 hours, then recovered toward $62,500. The Fear and Greed Index stood at 22, or extreme fear. At publication, BTC traded at $62,167 and ETH at $1,739, both down 2.04%. Total market capitalization fell 1.19% to $2.23 trillion.

Twenty-four-hour liquidations reached $331 million, including $262 million in longs, nearly 80% of the total, and $69.15 million in shorts. Shorts made up a larger share in the final hour as traders chasing the decline were squeezed. Liquidations on both sides point to volatility without a sustained one-way move.

BNB Chain plans a Layer 1 for AI agents and high-frequency trading, targeting a 2027 mainnet

The Block and Decrypt reported on July 8 that BNB Chain was developing a new layer-1 network separate from BSC for AI agents and frequent automated payments. TxStream is designed to send transactions directly to block producers to reduce exposure to sandwich attacks. PriorityLane would reserve capacity for oracles, liquidations and bridges. Targets include preconfirmation under 50 milliseconds, over 100,000 transactions per second and finality under one second. A testnet was scheduled for late 2026 and mainnet for early 2027.

The project is intended to complement BSC by serving use cases outside the older network's design. It remained at an early planning stage. The team had named neither a project lead nor any perpetuals or derivatives deployments.
