# Editorial review
Date: 2026-08-26 · Issue 59

## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: Arcus’ transferable positions appear alongside ATLAS’s backend services. This indicates that trading products are splitting asset formats, distributing entry points, and managing infrastructure.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: pTokens package perpetual trading positions as ERC-20 tokens. However, the risks borne by holders are different from those of LP shares. Distribution convenience cannot replace asset qualification review.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: The focus has shifted from comparing direct perpetual order entry points to comparing leverage shares held in wallets. The use of USDT as the sole collateral is maintained, and the applicability of collateral is not increased due to transferability.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: Priority should be given to analyzing competing products that allow for transferable leverage units. Development efforts for mortgage-based integration will be postponed for now. In terms of cooperation with the trading backend, it is necessary to first verify the production availability, risk responsibilities, and customer relationship boundaries.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If pTokens continue to have no external wallets or protocols, or if the net value deviates, redemption fails, or liquidation losses make the operating costs exceed the direct opening of positions, the distribution advantage needs to be downgraded.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: The promotional numbers of Arcus, Toobit’s historical yields, and regulatory opinions that were repeated yesterday have been deleted. Only events with mechanisms for incremental increases and clear boundaries should be retained.
