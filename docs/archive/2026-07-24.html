Mechanism: Pre-IPO equities such as SpaceX and individual semiconductor stocks drove much of the growth. Binance, Hyperliquid and OKX handled more than 80% of June volume, with Binance near half. Hyperliquid was the largest onchain venue in that group.

AFX Trade reports bridge vault losses involving compromised signing authority

Event: On day ten of coverage since July 15, Arbitrum-based perpetuals exchange AFX Trade reported losses of about 24.15 million USDC on July 22, close to its entire TVL. The funds were subsequently converted to roughly 12,467 ETH.

Mechanism: The Arbitrum native bridge and AFX bridge contract behaved as designed. The reported failure concerned the custody of AFX's own validator credentials.

Second-order effects: After Ostium's oracle incident, AFX was the second perpetuals DEX in a week to suffer losses close to the value of an entire vault following a failure in offchain access controls. A contract audit alone cannot establish whether privileged signing credentials are held securely.

ScopeThe incident affected AFX's custodial bridge. The Arbitrum native bridge was unaffected. AFX said its trading infrastructure and mainnet were not compromised.

BitMEX plans a September shutdown with reduce-only trading before mandatory settlement

BitMEX announced on July 23 that it had stopped accepting new registrations and would close the exchange on September 23 at 04:00 UTC. From August 26, traders could reduce positions but could not open new ones. Remaining positions would be closed in stages, and illiquid contracts could settle early.

The parent company described an orderly exit following a strategic review. It disclosed no hack or insolvency. Even an exchange whose product design is widely adopted can face pressure from liquidity, distribution and compliance costs. Market closure rules therefore matter throughout a derivatives venue's life.

ScopeBitMEX said its reserves exceeded liabilities and that customers had lost no funds to hacking in 11 years. The closure followed a strategic review. The company did not report a solvency crisis.

Benefits: A reliable spot reference, an identifiable catalyst and an existing distribution channel provide stronger grounds for a listing and for sustained price discovery.

Limitations: Event-driven markets can produce high turnover without durable open interest. Cross-venue volume is not a direct measure of demand for any one protocol.

Offchain signing authority warrants an independent review in bridge audits

Benefits: Reviewing the authority of signers, keepers, price publishers and bridge validators separately can reveal shared operational failures that contract audits alone miss.

Limitations: A higher signing threshold offers limited independence when keys share the same online environment and operational ownership.

An orderly market exit requires reduce-only trading and early settlement rules

Benefits: Reduce-only trading followed by staged settlement can limit congestion and price gaps during a market closure.

Limitations: Forced closure still leaves users exposed to limited liquidity and a fixed exit window. Clear rules cannot remove realized losses.
