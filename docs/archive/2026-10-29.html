Protocol design and user acquisition

Pacifica allocates 10 million points per week to reward organic activity

Pacifica takes a weekly activity snapshot at 00:00 UTC on Thursday and distributes 10 million points within 24 hours. Organic trading through its interface or API, user feedback and promotion may count. The formula changes and is undisclosed. Points cannot be bought, and anti-abuse rules prevent traders from converting a given volume into a guaranteed reward.

GRVT links its hybrid execution architecture to a weekly points program

GRVT matches orders and stores data offchain. It records trade settlement, liquidation checks and transfers on its own chain and submits zero-knowledge proofs to Ethereum. Of weekly reward points, 50% go to volume, 15% to open interest and 20% to direct referrals. The design rewards both trading and sustained exposure.

Paradex uses encrypted state to conceal positions and retains committee-based recovery

Paradex conceals orders, positions, P&L and liquidation prices, then submits encrypted state differences and zero-knowledge proofs to Ethereum. Users gain privacy and unified margin. An emergency exit still requires a committee comprising the Paradex Foundation, Paradex and Karnot to decrypt and publish state, leaving recovery dependent on governance.
