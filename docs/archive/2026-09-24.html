CFTC restricts event contracts on word mentions that pose high manipulation risks

Event: On September 22, the CFTC’s market surveillance department issued a consultation, stating that contracts based on whether individuals mention certain words, attend events, or interact with others pose a higher risk of manipulation.

Mechanism: Interested parties may directly influence these outcomes, and independent verification may be unavailable. Designated contract markets may list them only under limited conditions and must submit a full manipulation-resistance analysis for each contract. This narrows the prediction-market rules story tracked since July 24 from broad self-certification limits to specific high-risk contract types.

Measurement limitsThe consultation does not completely ban the use of contract-like instruments. Instead, it restricts their use and strengthens analysis based on individual contracts. Ultimately, federal and state-level regulations remain unresolved.
