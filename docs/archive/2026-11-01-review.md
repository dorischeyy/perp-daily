# Perp DEX Daily editorial review · 2026-11-01

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Binance’s attack involves backend authorization, withdrawal recovery, reserve replenishment, and futures insurance funds. This allows for an immediate assessment of how the trading platform separates user balances from its own losses.

**What is the most important finding?**: The loss of approximately $6.3 billion was not handled solely by SAFU. Binance also used corporate funds and some futures insurance funds to compensate for the losses. Having recovered 90% of assets does not mean that withdrawals, complete proof, and technical analysis are complete.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: It’s important to note that the focus has shifted from “off-chain privilege attacks mainly affecting bridges and state machines” to “large exchanges’ service container authorization chains can also cause cross-chain losses, and recovery processes will also involve derivatives risk buffers.”

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating trading platforms or self-built execution systems, it’s important to prioritize verifying backend authorization, withdrawal pauses, reserve replenishment, insurance fund calls, fault replaying, and public reviews. It’s not enough to just check if the private key has been leaked.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: Reassess the scope of the risk and the quality of recovery if a complete forensic investigation rules out a breach of back-office authorization, or if withdrawals, proof of reserves and insurance-fund replenishment are not completed as announced.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Candidates related to exchange expansion and market price fluctuations have been removed. Only facts that can affect security, collateral, pricing, and execution are retained.
