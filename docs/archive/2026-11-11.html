Funding channels and collateral efficiency

November 10: Binance halts transfers to entities linked to MEXC

Since November 10, Binance has rejected outbound transfers to MEXC-linked entities and held incoming deposits from them for review. FinCEN’s order appeared in the Federal Register that day and takes effect December 10. Trading remains available on MEXC, but moving funds across platforms has become more difficult ahead of the legal deadline.

November 10: Binance announces higher portfolio-margin collateral ratios for FIL and HBAR

On November 13, Binance will raise the collateral ratios for FIL and HBAR from 50% to 60% for cross-margin borrowing and transfers out, and for portfolio margin. It will also raise tier limits for professional accounts. These changes affect borrowing capacity and the unified maintenance margin ratio. Users can make better use of collateral but must recalculate their buffer against liquidation.

Research on perpetuals mechanics

GRVT matches orders offchain, requires onchain authorization to move funds and transfers liquidations to its insurance fund

GRVT matches orders through a centralized service, validates trades, transfers and liquidations on its own chain, and submits zero-knowledge proofs to Ethereum. Each action that affects funds requires a signature from the user’s registered wallet. At liquidation, the insurance fund takes over the entire isolated-margin position or the entire cross-margin account. Order execution and control of funds thus sit at different layers.

November 10: Scale AI measures when GRPO catches up with PPO on long agent tasks

Scale AI found that longer, more complex tasks require larger batches for GRPO to match PPO with a critic. In a thousand-step agent trajectory, a single success-or-failure reward spreads credit across many actions. The researchers recommend weighing batch size against the cost of a critic. With a small budget, PPO achieved comparable results in fewer GPU-hours.
