Reports dated July 24 regarding AFX and BitMEX are not included in this summary; only the main points and actions are summarized, without copying the numerical details from the original text.
6. **Newcomers**: The text explains why it’s necessary to reduce positions first before opening new ones, why continuous trading isn’t suitable for energy perpetual contracts, and why products with expiration dates still use the “perp” designation.
