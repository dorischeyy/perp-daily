New markets and unified clearing

November 2: MEXC adds five U.S. equity perpetuals and reserves discretion over corporate actions

At 14:00 UTC on November 2, MEXC listed perpetuals on GPN, NEE, NTRA, DLR and CVNA with up to 20x leverage in cross or isolated margin. Corporate actions can trigger early settlement or position adjustments. Trading and liquidations pause during circuit breakers, but users can still cancel existing orders.

November 2: Eurex Clearing announces a hybrid central counterparty for traditional and natively digital securities

Eurex Clearing announced plans to bring traditional and natively digital securities under the same rulebook and risk model. The first ESM digital instruments are intended to trade on Eurex Repo, clear through a common central counterparty and qualify as margin collateral. The proposal avoids running two separate capital pools. Production scale and the launch date have yet to be disclosed.

Trading distribution and brokerage infrastructure

Trasia connects HyperCore and HIP-3 markets in a single mobile app

Trasia’s mobile app supports crypto perpetuals, HIP-3 equity, index, commodity and FX markets, and limit, stop-loss and TWAP orders. Accounts remain noncustodial, and users can switch between standard and unified account modes. Google Play showed only 50-plus downloads, leaving the reach of the app unproven.

Hypro uses builder fees to include third-party Hyperliquid interface charges in orders

Hypro offers access to Hyperliquid perpetuals, spot and HIP-3 markets. New users who sign up through Hypro’s referral can avoid the additional fee. Users with an existing referral must authorize a builder fee of up to 0.05%. The actual fee is 0.01% on eligible fills and settles onchain with the order. Spot purchases carry no fee.

Talos offers configurable pricing, hedging and client records for white-label brokerages

Institutions can use Talos’ white-label services to quote prices for their clients and set spreads. Orders can be automatically hedged among more than 100 liquidity providers. Client balances are maintained in internal ledgers. Compared to directly calling exchange APIs, this solution covers both the front and back ends of brokerage operations. However, licensing, client identity verification, and compliance responsibilities remain the responsibility of the institutions.

AI products and developer tools

November 2: OpenAI releases LifeVal to evaluate everyday tasks

OpenAI released LifeVal, which uses time-use research and surveys conducted with 3,995 participants to evaluate tasks related to parenting, health, education, household management, finance, shopping, and government affairs. It assesses whether agents can complete tasks from start to finish. LifeVal shifts the evaluation from knowledge-based questions to real-world actions. However, the results are mainly based on the official model, and external verification is still required.

Vercel MCP can display deployment status, build logs, source code, and submission information on chat clients that support MCP Apps. After deployment, users can directly access these resources. Users can also send logs for troubleshooting errors. Clients that do not support interactive cards still return text status and preview links.

November 2: Vercel adds support for configuring projects with vercel.toml

Vercel projects can now use vercel.toml in place of vercel.json or vercel.ts. It supports the same settings, adds comments and offers a flatter structure for roots, entry points and rewrites in multiservice projects. A project can use only one configuration file, so a migration must resolve conflicts between coexisting files.
