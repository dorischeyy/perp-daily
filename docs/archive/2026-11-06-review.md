# Perp DEX Daily editorial review

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Hosting, execution, and exit are becoming new competitive aspects of perpetual platforms. Dividends from stock perpetuals also show that rights settlement does not equal holding physical assets.

**What is the most important finding?**: Bitget mirrors externally custodied assets into spot, margin and futures accounts. It has yet to disclose settlement times for mirrored balances or deadlines for withdrawing during disruptions. Those details merit further verification alongside its claim of lower custody risk.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: Shift from “non-crypto perpetuals with benchmarks and leverage” to “hosted positions, company actions, market close prices, network recovery, and collateral plans need to be evaluated separately.”

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating similar products, those that are already available, planned to be launched, tradable, capable of token distribution, and independently verifiable can be separated from those that are not. No single status can summarize all usability.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: The maturity assessment would rise if externally custodied mirror balances could settle immediately during liquidations or disruptions, corporate actions could flow through to equity perpetuals automatically without delay, or the planned perpetuals and collateral functions had actually opened.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Ordinary expansion items and version updates that do not change execution, hosting, rights, exits, or Agent control have been deleted.

### Six reader perspectives

- Products: External hosting, exchange execution, and asset mirroring are separated. Changes in hosting locations are not considered as unconditional risk reduction.
- Trading: A leverage ratio of 2000 times applies only to nominal positions of up to 50,000 USDT. Leverage may be temporarily reduced before an event, but this does not translate into fixed full-position parameters.
- Risks: Dividend replenishment, coin recovery, and planned collateral retain their time and status boundaries. The progress of announcements is not written as final completion.
- Newcomers: Bullbit migration only explains the account where funds are located, session key permissions, and the impact of old API keys. It does not stack chain architecture terms.
- Operations: Assets on six networks were restored in batches. The phrase “more assets” was no longer used to refer to all assets that could be withdrawn.
- Engineering: The three AI mechanisms retain restricted access, preview status, and false positive delays. Manufacturer tests are not considered independent production verifications.
