**What is the most important finding?**: Ripple Prime integrates onchain RWA perpetuals into institutions' existing credit, cross-margin and clearing arrangements.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The TradFi and RWA perpetuals story has progressed from new listings to institutional prime brokerage access. **What decision can readers make?**: For perpetuals access, verify execution, margin and prime broker responsibilities separately. For agent deployment, require runtime revocation of permissions and an audit trail.

**Which news recap was removed?**: The duplicate stocks trading contracts on Bybit, old results from ChainGPT, tokenized stocks that are duplicated in OKX, and European adoption proposals from OpenAI without any new mechanisms have been deleted.

### Six reader perspectives

1. Senior traders: Ripple Prime and OKX have changed the interfaces for accounts and margin trading. However, neither platform offers real-time trading, OI, spreads, or settlement capabilities. Therefore, accessibility cannot be equated with actual execution.
2. Competing product lead: Institutional prime brokerage, unified self-custody accounts and DEX execution costs measure distribution, account design and liquidity, respectively. A listing count misses all three.
3. CEO and strategy: Stablecoins and tokenized assets are entering existing networks, brokerage firms, and private credit platforms. The real barriers are existing customer relationships, liability assumption, and compliance in transfers.
4. Skeptics: PerpFinder, DappRadar, and MemeFees are all aggregate or snapshot-based systems. The text must retain the timing, missing items, and boundaries that cannot be extrapolated.
5. Editor: Each of the four sections has three entries. The lead focuses on institutional perpetuals, unified accounts and agent permissions. Routine listings and financing announcements were excluded.
6. For new users: events and mechanisms are separated. The context clearly indicates plans, production, beta testing, snapshots, and adoption gaps. No additional product judgment columns are required.
