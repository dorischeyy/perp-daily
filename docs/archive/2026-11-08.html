Perpetual market design

GMX liquidation checks account for minimum collateral, fees and price impact

GMX tests liquidation against the higher of a percentage threshold and an absolute collateral floor, accounting for accrued fees and capped negative price impact. Linked stop orders or collateral top-ups receive only best-effort priority. When a pool owes excessive profits to traders, the protocol can auto-deleverage profitable positions. It may also block LP redemptions temporarily when trader P&L exceeds a specified share of the pool.
