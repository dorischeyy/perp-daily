Regulatory access and shared trading infrastructure

On November 19th, the CFTC clarified two licensed pathways for Hyperliquid to enter the US retail market.

CFTC Chair Michael Selig said that onchain venues such as Hyperliquid must register as an exchange or partner with one already registered to offer derivatives to U.S. retail users. He added that single-stock perpetuals could qualify as futures depending on their structure, and compared daily funding to variation margin on futures.
