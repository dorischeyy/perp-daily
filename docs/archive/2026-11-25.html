Nov 23: CFTC phases in reporting rules for fully collateralized event contracts

On November 23, the CFTC adopted a final rule moving eligible fully collateralized event contracts from certain swap-reporting and no-action arrangements into the futures and options framework under Parts 15 through 18, with new Section 16.03. Markets, futures commission merchants, clearing members and foreign brokers face a phased transition with deadlines of 60 days, no later than July 26, 2027, and 12 months, depending on the obligation.

CFTC staff outline haircuts and liquidity limits for crypto assets used as operating resources

CFTC staff said that swap execution facilities, designated contract markets and derivatives clearing organizations may count BTC, ETH and payment stablecoins toward resources covering at least 12 months of operating costs. The indicative minimum haircuts are 20%, 20% and 2%, respectively. These assets do not qualify as cash or highly liquid securities. Venues must still meet separate requirements for three or six months of liquid resources. The staff view is neither a new rule nor a no-action commitment.

Pacifica distinguishes market liquidation, backstop and auto-deleveraging

Pacifica first reduces a position using market orders. If market depth is insufficient, the liquidation vault takes the remainder. Only the last stage automatically deleverages profitable counterparties. Account balance history distinguishes market, backstop and ADL events. The market API also exposes each asset’s maximum leverage, minimum order size and hourly funding rate, giving trading interfaces the information to show how the rules apply to each market.
