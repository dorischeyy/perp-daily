Perpetual pricing and execution

October 29: Bybit details closed-market index smoothing and treatment of the SOXX stock split

Bybit will apply an exponentially weighted moving average to 40 traditional-asset indices. Its half-life is 120 seconds in premarket and after-hours sessions and 600 seconds when the market is closed. During the SOXX split, Bybit will pause trading, divide the mark price by three, multiply contract quantity by three and skip funding for the adjustment window. Corporate actions and closed-market pricing require separate treatment.
