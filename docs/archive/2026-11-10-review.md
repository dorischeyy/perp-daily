# Editorial review

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: On the same day, OKX announced the integration of tokenized stock trading and the handling of stock splits. The order book, company actions, perpetual profit and loss confirmation, and protection orders are now part of a complete asset service chain.

**What is the most important finding?**: SNDK split does not simply involve dividing the price by eight. In old warehouses, profits and losses will be calculated first, while in new warehouses, operations will resume at the adjusted mark price. Stop-loss orders and some remaining orders will need to be re-established separately.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: RWA competition has been expanded to include quantity-based targets, trading hours, and rights mapping. It also includes unified order books, as well as positions, orders, and API continuity during company actions.

**What decision can readers make?**: Products and risk management can prioritize verifying position reconstruction, protection orders, historical profits and losses, and API order identifiers under company actions such as stock splits and dividends. Instead of using “support for RWA,” a complete acceptance process should be adopted.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If unified assets do not provide cross-issuer liquidity aggregation, or if there are errors, order losses, recovery delays, or asset deviations during the SNDK adjustments on November 20th, the conclusions regarding the enforceability of unified rules should be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Patches for perpetual contracts, meeting notifications, and non-mechanized increments have been removed. Only those changes that affect asset services or Agent operation boundaries will be retained.

Avantis belongs to the perp-protocol category. It uses USDC wallets to manage traders’ profits and losses, with risk adjustment via skewed incentives. OKX’s tokenized stocks belong to the spot-infrastructure and centralized trading asset services category. SNDK’s perpetual and X-Perp rules belong to the derivatives company’s actions. The main evidence for Avantis comes from official loss refunds, avUSDC vaults, and risk management documents. It can verify the triggering of refunds, position locking, and relationships between vault counterparties. In the past 30 days, no independent trader materials that can be verified and quantify real arbitrage gains, adoption rates, or improvements in vaults under extreme market conditions have been found. Therefore, discussions are left blank, and the discussion_gap is clearly stated. The collaboration with protocol partners is not considered an independent validation.

### New findings and open questions

There were no studies focusing on Avantis’ reverse losses in the past seven periods. This incremental update combines traders’ return positions, vault counterparties, and LP buffer connections into a single risk allocation path. It is necessary to examine historical changes in real-time return ratios for various assets, subsidy costs, usage rates of independent traders, improvements in vault withdrawal, and the actual implementation of returns and funding arbitrage opportunities.
