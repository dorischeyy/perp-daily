CME withdraws its proposal for round-the-clock micro crude oil futures

Event: CME has withdrawn its original plans to launch 24/7 crude oil futures in 10 barrels. Industry participants are concerned that these products may introduce unexpected risks.

Mechanism: CME had positioned the product as a regulated alternative to onchain prediction markets and offshore perpetuals. Its voluntary withdrawal shifts the competitive question from technical feasibility to eligible assets, liquidity and responsibility for risk. This updates the U.S. perpetuals story tracked since June 19.

ImplementationThis is an initiative by the CME to suspend and withdraw its proposal. It is not a ban by the CFTC, nor is it a court ruling determining whether perpetual contracts are considered swaps or futures. The legal proceedings remain open.

SEC proposes conditional path for institutional crypto self-custody

Event: The SEC has proposed new rules that allow registered investment advisors and regulated funds to self-hold crypto assets under certain conditions. It also allows qualified state-level trust companies to act as custodians.

Mechanism: The proposal updates financial audits, brokerage custody, recording, and disclosure requirements. This changes the situation where qualified custodians were previously lacking, turning it into a condition that can be reviewed.

ImplementationThe formal proposal requires a 60-day public comment period after publication in the Federal Register. The scope of self-custody and final safeguards may still change.

zkAPI separates the payer’s identity from the AI prompt

Event: The Ethereum Foundation and Open Anonymity have launched a zkAPI that is already operational on the main network. Users can deposit ETH or USDC into a vault, and then use zero-knowledge proofs to obtain short-term API keys with a maximum limit.

Mechanism: Payment servers only verify balances and expenditures. Model providers receive prompts but do not know the identity of the corresponding deposits. After the session ends, settlement is done using a signed receipt, ensuring that the entire session can be proven at once.

ScopezkAPI hides payment-related information, but does not hide IP addresses, time patterns, or message content. Stable network identifiers and recognizable text may still allow different sessions to be reconnected.
