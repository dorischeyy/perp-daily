Arcus makes shares in perpetual strategies transferable as pTokens

Event: The Block, citing the team, reported that Arcus on Robinhood Chain introduced pTokens. These ERC-20 tokens represent shares in perpetual positions held in strategy accounts, with exposure to crypto and stocks including HOOD.

Mechanism: Each token corresponds to a particular market and leverage strategy. A transfer moves a share of the strategy account rather than the holder’s own margin account. Funding and liquidation risk remain with the underlying strategy even though the token can move between wallets.

Second-order effects: The product changes how positions can be distributed. A spot-style interface may simplify access, but adoption as collateral elsewhere will depend on how the shares are valued, redeemed and disclosed.

Measurement limitsThe report established the token structure and gave product examples. It did not establish acceptance by lending protocols. Trading figures varied between versions of the source and are excluded here.

LayerZero announces ATLAS and plans trading back-office services for brokers

Event: LayerZero Labs announced ATLAS, trading infrastructure for brokers and platforms planned for later this year. Partners would retain their own applications and customer relationships.

Mechanism: ATLAS would combine matching, clearing, settlement and risk controls in one engine. Zero would supply verification for both open markets and institutional markets with admission requirements.

Second-order effects: If ATLAS goes live, new trading interfaces could buy an integrated backend, further separating customer distribution from trading infrastructure. Performance, reliability and the division of responsibilities still require production evidence.

Measurement limitsThe announcement describes a planned launch. The venue is not yet live. Stress-test latency cannot establish performance under real loads, recovery from failures, or institutional adoption.

Volume and open interest decline across four derivatives venues

Event: In the August 26 snapshot at 07:47 Beijing time, combined 24-hour volume across Binance, Hyperliquid, Bybit and Gate fell 6.7%, from about 1,622,400 BTC to 1,514,100 BTC. Open interest fell 1.5%, from roughly 863,700 BTC to 850,900 BTC.

Mechanism: Hyperliquid volume rose slightly while volume at the other three venues declined. The lower overall figure therefore does not establish a corresponding fall in demand at the onchain venue.

Second-order effects: The previous day’s volume rebound did not persist across this sample. Venue migration and changes to outstanding positions need separate tracking. Falling BTC-denominated open interest alone cannot establish dollar exposure or the amount liquidated.

Measurement limitsBoth observations use the same four venues and BTC units, about 24 hours apart. Centralized venue figures include derivatives beyond perpetuals, and the sample does not represent all DEXs.

The service executes portfolio management within individual wallets instead of pooling assets in a fund. It is open only to non-U.S. investors in approved regions and remains subject to geographic restrictions, smart contract risk and rebalancing costs.

Measurement limitsThe announcement confirms the product structure and rollout plan. It does not establish that all products are tradable. The methodology fee has no stated period and is not presented as annual.
