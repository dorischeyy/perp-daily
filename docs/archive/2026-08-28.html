KuCoin UTA API V2 exposes machine-readable contract lifecycle states

Event: KuCoin said UTA API V2 took effect on August 27. V1 would remain available without new features until its expected retirement on December 31. WebSocket authentication moved from connection tokens to signatures.

Mechanism: V2 adds the pre-market conversion date, expiry and settlement times, maximum market-order size and leverage, funding data and the last rate. It distinguishes initialization, open trading, cancellation only, preparation for settlement, settling, settled, suspended and closed states.

Second-order effects: Trading interfaces, order systems and risk checks can use the same state definitions to distinguish halts and settlement from routine errors. These are KuCoin’s integration states. Onchain keeper, LP and liquidation systems may require different states.

ImplementationV2 had taken effect. V1’s retirement date remained a plan, and the migration status of individual integrators was not disclosed.

Event: On August 27, HTX listed WMT/USDT and SHEIN/USDT perpetuals with up to 20× leverage. The same round-the-clock derivatives interface now covers an established listed retailer and an apparel company approaching its IPO.

Bitrue delists its ASTEROID perpetual and settles remaining positions at market prices

Event: Bitrue scheduled the ASTEROID/USDT perpetual for delisting at 08:00 UTC on August 28. Trading will stop and any positions still open will be processed at the market price.

Mechanism: A fixed delisting time concentrates the deadline for voluntary closure, order cancellation, settlement and final price determination. Showing only that trading has stopped can cause users to mistake scheduled settlement for an unexpected liquidation.

Second-order effects: For smaller contracts, market quality includes advance notice of the time, pricing and treatment of positions on exit as well as depth after launch. One delisting cannot establish an industry-wide retreat.

ScopeThe notice applies only to ASTEROID/USDT. Market-price settlement follows the venue's delisting rules and is distinct from a normal liquidation caused by insufficient margin.
