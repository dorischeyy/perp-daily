Bybit adds a dynamic minimum ask for short-dated options

Event: On September 24, Bybit adjusted the minimum ask price for contracts with two days or less until expiry. The change covers all crypto options and TradFi perpetual options.

Mechanism: The new lower limit is the maximum of a fixed floor price, short-term floor price, and dynamic risk floor price. The short-term floor price is 10% of the option’s mark price, to prevent low selling prices from breaking through risk controls near expiration.

ScopeThe rules only constrain the lowest bid for sell orders. The mark price, index price, Delta, current positions, and margin calculations remain unchanged.
