Aster directs 70% of grid rewards to unfilled resting orders

Event: From October 14, Aster revised grid liquidity mining so that 70% of rewards depend on resting order size, distance from the mark and time on the book. The remaining 30% depends on maker volume.

Mechanism: Unfilled resting orders can earn most of the rewards. Taker trades do not count. The incentive shifts from turnover to visible order book depth. Rewards are calculated hourly and distributed after a 24-hour delay.

Measurement limitsThe event lasts until October 25th. However, the announcement does not disclose any additional depth, improved spreads, or cost reductions associated with rewards. Therefore, it is not possible to confirm an increase in liquidity.
