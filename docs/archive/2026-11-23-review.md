## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Similar data shows that the number of perpetual DEXs has increased to 50, but Hyperliquid still accounts for 57.2% of open interest. Competition from new platforms has not yet changed the concentration of top players.
**What is the most important finding?**: Ostium and GMX have defined rules for market closure, failed price extraction, price impact, slippage protection, and LP exits, which can be verified directly. These rules can be used to calibrate the trading interface.
**Which prior assessment changed?**: The previous assumption that “new platforms will gradually diversify shares” has been downgraded in confidence. Instead, it is now believed that “execution boundaries and exit rules are more decisive in determining differences among platforms.”
**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating or designing a real-world asset perpetual, check whether users can submit or execute orders while the underlying market is closed, what a failed order costs, how price impact and slippage are defined, and when liquidity providers can withdraw.
**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If subsequent data shows a continuous decline in Hyperliquid market share, or if the official product practices and documentation of Ostium and GMX deviate significantly from previous standards, it will be necessary to re-evaluate the situation.
**Which news recap was removed?**: Deleted projects involving the launch, removal, and maintenance of regular contracts, as well as AI events that were repackaged from second-hand articles.

### Six reader perspectives

- Traders: When the Ostium market closes, traders can place orders with conditions, but they cannot place market orders. Whether transactions occur after the market opens is also subject to conditions and slippage. The slippage of GMX does not equal the impact on prices.
- Product Manager of Competitors: The interface must separate aspects such as placing orders, execution, failure fees, and fund withdrawal. The concept of “24/7 trading” cannot cover all variations in these aspects.
- CEO and strategy: None of the 50 platforms naturally brings diversification of shares. Resources should be prioritized towards verified execution quality and differentiated markets, rather than just increasing the number of projects.
- Skeptics: PerpsAtlas relies on third-party data sources. Ostium and GMX lack recent independent transaction samples; therefore, official documents cannot be equated with actual usage results.
- Editor’s Note: The trading section focuses solely on concentration and execution boundaries. The AI-related section only includes three official changes or deployments; news-related content is not included in the list.
- New rules: Each number should retain its time, unit, and caliber. When the research document does not have a publication date, it should only record the verification date, without pretending to be an event of the same day.
