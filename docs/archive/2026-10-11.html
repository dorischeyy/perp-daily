Event: On October 9, the CFTC proposed clarifying that event contracts on sports, politics, culture and weather may fall within the definition of a swap. A related interim final rule excludes casino-style gambling from that definition. The federal prediction market story tracked since July 24 now has formal text to examine.

Mechanism: If swap-related contracts are included in the definition, the boundaries of federal derivatives regulation will become clearer. However, the proposed rule still has a 30-day comment period. The conflict between state-level gambling regulations and federal commodity laws has not yet been resolved. The accompanying rules are in the process of becoming effective, and the legal status of these two documents differs.

ImplementationEvent contracts are included in swaps as proposed rules. Casino-style gambling is excluded from the accompanying temporary final rules. Neither can be considered completed legislation.
