01

Contract lifecycles and external dependencies

On November 16th, Bitget Wallet revealed that three perpetual exchanges use different SNDK splitting schemes.

Bitget Wallet’s SNDK stock-split notice shows that PopDEX and Hyperliquid will suspend trading and forcibly settle remaining positions. tatadex will keep positions open, multiply position size by eight and divide average entry price by eight. All three venues will cancel unfilled orders, including take-profit and stop-loss orders. The same wallet can therefore expose users to different contract lifecycles.
