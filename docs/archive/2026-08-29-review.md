## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: Gate for the first time included daily 2x reset ETFs in the highest 20x equity perpetual futures. HTX uses negative resting orders to compete for TradFi transactions. The expansion of order streams and order subsidies begin to overlap.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: Leveraged ETFs are not just ordinary stocks with a different code. The daily reset path of underlying assets, along with external perpetual leverage, contribute to price, margin, and settlement risks.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: Assess daily-reset leveraged ETF perpetuals separately for underlying leverage, cumulative tracking differences and price gaps during market closures, in addition to the contract leverage and trading hours used for single-stock perpetuals.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: Products and risk control should first place leveraged ETFs in an independent asset profile. After verifying the price source, extreme gaps, and cumulative settlement effects, a decision can be made regarding the launch, as well as setting limits on leverage and OI.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: The case for a separate risk tier would weaken if live data showed that leveraged ETF perpetuals had no greater mispricing, liquidation exposure or loss of liquidity than ordinary single-stock contracts across sessions and during extreme volatility.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: Deleted candidates with only activity names, single coin codes, or brand partnerships, without mechanisms, limits, or verification boundaries.
