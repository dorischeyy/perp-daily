**What is the most important finding?**: Extended has transferred approximately $120 million in TVL, existing positions, and orders without any interruptions in trading. Verifying the trading status can be separated from the underlying settlement network.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating a new settlement chain or RWA perpetual contract, it is necessary to assess asset migration, order continuity, the oracle, suspension status, and exit channels separately.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If there are undisclosed positions, orders, or redemption anomalies after the migration, or if the multi-platform market on Arc continues to lack depth, the current assessment needs to be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Data related to token launches, financing, token prices, and duplicate regulatory information that did not add anything new during this period has been removed.
