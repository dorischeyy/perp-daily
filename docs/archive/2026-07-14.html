RWA perpetuals reach records onchain and on centralized exchanges

Perp DEX

Equity and commodity perpetuals on HIP-3 grow from 2% to 50% of Hyperliquid's volume this year

Event: On July 13, The Block reported that HIP-3 stock and commodity perpetuals accounted for about 50% of Hyperliquid volume, up from roughly 2% at the start of the year, a 25-fold increase in share. CryptoTimes reported a 2026 open-interest high above $11.07 billion. The edition’s snapshot put it near $10.8 billion. Real-world asset contracts reached $3.6 billion in open interest, and HIP-3 contributed around $3.69 billion, approaching the $4 billion level tracked since June 12. This was day 32 of coverage.

Mechanism: HIP-3 allows independent builders to turn assets with observable prices, including stocks, commodities and indices, into 24/7 leveraged markets. Positions have no scheduled expiry and exchange funding payments. Trade.xyz accounts for most open interest on this layer.

Second-order effects: With about half of volume now linked to non-crypto assets, HIP-3 has changed Hyperliquid’s business mix. Its growth also exposes concentration in a single builder. A change in builder share or settlement standards could alter the competitive position even if the range of listed assets remains wide.

Robinhood Chain reaches the top five by weekly DEX volume while real-world assets account for only 4% of TVL

Event: The Block reported on July 13 that Bernstein estimated $3.1 billion in DEX volume and about 65,000 active users during Robinhood Chain's first seven days. On one day it ranked third behind Solana and BNB Chain at $809 million, putting it among the five largest chains by DEX volume. CoinDesk found that after 13 days only 4.1% of $312 million in TVL was tied to RWAs: $12.81 million, including $10.68 million in stock tokens. Asset management and lending represented nearly 79%. CASHCAT alone was worth $156 million, more than ten times the chain's RWA assets. Third-party bridge Relay also reported missing token purchases in some wallets. Other balances and private keys were unaffected. The scale was undisclosed. This is day 12 of coverage since July 2.

Mechanism: Bernstein's top-five ranking includes general DEX trading, mostly spot swaps, and does not separate perpetuals activity from memecoin speculation. Arcus's promised 35 RWA perpetuals markets had yet to open for trading since the July 1 launch.

Second-order effects: Several independent measures show audience reach and DEX volume growing faster than delivery of the promised RWA contracts. If Relay confirms a material loss, the incident could undermine Robinhood Chain's claim to brokerage-grade security.

June real-world asset perpetual volume on centralized exchanges rises 57% to a record $311 billion

Event: CoinDesk exchange data cited by CryptoSlate on July 13 showed June RWA perpetuals volume on crypto exchanges rose 57% from May to a record $311 billion. Binance accounted for 78.6%, about $245 billion. The category, which includes non-crypto assets such as stocks and commodities, was negligible at year-end 2025. This is day five of coverage since July 9.

Mechanism: Binance Research's July 8 report put first-half volume on stablecoin-settled traditional-asset perpetuals at $1.1 trillion and their share of crypto perpetuals at roughly 11%. That market-wide figure covers a longer period than the June centralized-exchange figure. Together they indicate that this category has grown beyond isolated launches.

Second-order effects: Binance's 78.6% share in RWA perpetuals exceeds its share in conventional crypto perpetuals. Its liquidity and brand could give it an advantage over onchain venues unless those venues offer better listings, leverage terms or fees.

Bitcoin falls below $62,400 as implied odds of a July Fed rate increase rise from 10% to 50%

Event: CoinDesk reported on July 14 that markets raised the implied chance of a July Fed rate increase from 10% a few days earlier to around 50% after Governor Christopher Waller said higher rates might be needed to contain inflation. BTC fell more than 2% in 24 hours to about $62,400, consistent with the daily snapshot's 2.93% drop. The two-year Treasury yield reached a year-to-date high of 4.29%. Economists expected June CPI, due later that day, could come in below 4% year over year.

Mechanism: Expectations of higher short-term rates make fixed-income assets relatively more attractive and can weigh on non-yielding assets such as bitcoin. The move fits recent observations that rates and liquidity have had more influence on BTC than geopolitical headlines.
