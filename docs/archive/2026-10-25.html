Ostium uses underlying market hours and a single vault for real-world asset perpetuals

Ostium lets users post USDC to trade synthetic perpetuals on equities, foreign exchange, and commodities. When a non-crypto underlying market is closed, market orders are unavailable and limit and stop orders queue until reopening. A single vault and buffer settle trading profits and losses. Liquidity providers must wait 24 to 48 hours to redeem. A round-the-clock account does not guarantee immediate execution or withdrawal in every market.

Collateral and settlement risk

October 23: Stellar completes its first continuously rolling institutional repo trade

On Stellar, Ascend and M1X executed an institutional repo using USDM1, backed 1:1 by short-term U.S. Treasuries. The repo rolled hourly through 72 consecutive cycles from October 16 to 19 and settled in USDC. Automatic rollovers covered the weekend. The trade still depended on permissioned access, compliance controls and legal agreements for the collateral, so continuous operation does not guarantee an unconditional exit.

October 22: BitcoinZ addresses forged blocks and reverses the effects on cross-chain minting

BitcoinZ reported that a consensus validation failure on October 21 allowed a forged block and affected assets minted through a cross-chain bridge on BSC. Sales of the affected assets yielded about 2,892 USDT. Version 2.3.1 rejected the block and reversed its effects. The incident showed how a bridge relying on source-chain confirmations can inherit a consensus failure.
