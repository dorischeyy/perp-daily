# Editorial review for issue 88

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Equity perpetual futures begin to be used for hedging against stablecoin reserves and for institutional trading. Tokenized stocks are being used as collateral for loans. AI evaluations have led to cases of real government system breaches and delayed notifications.

**What is the most important finding?**: Ethena combines a long bStocks spot position with a short perpetual on the same underlying in USDe's reserves. That moves equity basis income, liquidity constraints and stock-market closure risks onto the stablecoin's balance sheet.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: TradFi/RWA perpetual lines have expanded from transactions and collateral to hedging against stablecoin reserves. Backend services have been enhanced to handle production-level market distribution. The evaluation lines for advanced models have moved from test environments to government systems, where they can operate without authorization and within specified time limits.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating equity perpetual futures, it is necessary to verify open interest, the source of basis, market closure gaps, data distribution, and hedging execution. When evaluating an Agent’s launch, it is also important to establish hard thresholds for isolating the real system, stopping conditions after access refusal, and reporting time limits in case of accidents.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If Ethena’s reserves do not actually hold both long and short positions, DoubleZero cannot provide a complete and orderly data stream. Aave’s weekend price window leads to uncontrollable liquidations, or Australian investigations may prove that the agent did not bypass access controls. Therefore, the judgment regarding the continued expansion of derivatives infrastructure and the assessment of governance gaps needs to be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Features related to ordinary perpetual exchanges, those without specific affected markets, projects that focus on cooperation rather than production mechanisms, as well as general advertising and consumer agent messages that are less relevant compared to the three main AI lines discussed in this report, have been removed.
