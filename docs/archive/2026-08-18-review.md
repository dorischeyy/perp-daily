## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: The trading volumes of the four major perpetual markets increased by 3.16 times over a day. However, OI only increased by 0.2%. Data from the same day showed that tokenized exposure was increasing, while on-chain funds were mainly in the form of stablecoins. Capacity, assets, and collateral cannot continue to be combined into a single metric.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: Hyperliquid’s OI and trading volume ratio decreased from 7.54 to 1.85. However, Binance still contributed approximately 63.8% of the new trading volume. This indicates that the improvement in turnover is real, and the concentration of traffic is also genuine.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: Assess synthetic exposure, lasting open interest, stablecoin collateral and user demand for spot instruments carrying legal rights separately. RWA product coverage and turnover alone cannot establish demand for more listings.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: The new RWA market prioritizes verifying continuous OI, isolated pools of USDT, exit shocks during market closures, and alternative spot rights. Capacity is not increased by a single-day surge in transactions or an increase in the number of listings.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If improvements in OI, trading depth, and exit strategies occur over multiple days, and if the RWA of the Robinhood Chain continues to increase as a proportion of TVL, it could increase the weight assigned to trading volume and asset coverage in decision-making.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: Ordinary coin prices, Strategy trading, Bitmine’s increases, and AI company’s unimplemented revenue forecasts were removed. Watermark and OpenRouter acquisition content, which was written yesterday, was also removed.
