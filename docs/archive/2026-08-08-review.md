# Editorial review

## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: Bybit expanded its private company pre-market perpetual futures and public equity perpetual futures. Gate’s total OI exceeded Hyperliquid’s, and asset expansion, lifecycle rules, and risk capacity were compared on the same day.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: MOONSHOTUSDT has transformed an AI company that has not yet gone public into a leveraged price. The exchange must define the contract states during listing, postponement, cancellation, and when the price source becomes invalid in advance.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: The unified asset-level launch mechanism for equity perpetual futures has been changed to separate lifecycle mechanisms for pre-market or private company assets. Public stock templates cannot be reused.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: Priority is given to assets that can be verified through pre-market events, leverage reduction, suspension, and settlement rules. Then, it is decided whether to follow competitors or not, rather than rushing to launch products based on speed.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If pre-market perpetual futures can continue to operate despite independent lifecycle rules, and they maintain low prices, non-disputed settlements, and high retention rates, then the priority of the state machine should be reduced.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: Removed the previous day's Meta configuration incident, the AISI report of 19 unauthorized actions, Cardano IBC and routine listings. The retained campaign entries state what delivery can actually be verified.

1. **Top traders/major players**: Pre-market perpetual futures clearly indicate the absence of public perpetual futures anchors. Gate and Hyperliquid only compare the OI and turnover across the entire platform in the same snapshot. They don’t consider size as a measure of liquidity.
2. **Responsible person for competing products.**: Bybit’s attempt to launch six US stocks in two days and introduce private company pre-market perpetual futures represents a real speed challenge. However, due to the lack of index, depth, and lifecycle rules, this approach cannot be directly replicated.
3. **CEO/Strategy**: Product priorities lie in reusable event state machines, price sources, and risk control mechanisms. The goal is not to create surface-level coverage with more assets.
4. **Skeptics**: Activities, elections, organizational adjustments, and model studies are all distinguished by announcements, execution, and external verification. Astra’s mathematical results are clearly kept for independent verification.
5. **Editor**: Three items per column. Perp DEX highlights the most significant competitive changes. Product decisions only consider those changes that will affect the order of priorities.
6. **Newcomers**: Pre-market perpetual futures, public equity perpetual futures, OI across the platform, event distribution, and on-chain appointments are all explained separately. No mixed reporting of different types of information is used.
