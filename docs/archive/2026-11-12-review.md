# Editorial review

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: xStocks listed more than 200 European stocks on the blockchain at once. Pyth provided real-time market data for five NYSE groups. RWA supply and official price data expanded on the same day.

**What is the most important finding?**: Alongside bids, offers and trades, Pyth distributes halt, resumption and short-sale restriction states. These fields can directly affect equity perpetuals risk controls around the underlying market's close and reopening.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The original judgment focused on RWA competition, new targets, and company actions. Now, updates are related to asset issuance, regulated custody, original exchange quotes, and distribution channels.

**What decision can readers make?**: Product and risk management teams can prioritize verifying information related to underlying stock custody, exchange status updates, pre-market equity resets, and front-end distribution responsibilities. The number of indicators can be used instead of complete asset service capabilities.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If new assets do not generate available liquidity, then NYSE data cannot reliably drive the suspension of trading. Additionally, media entries may not accurately reflect the actual order flow. Therefore, the assumption that infrastructure is expanding rapidly should be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Ordinary crypto perpetual contracts, adjustments to single tick sizes, repeated delisting announcements, and rumors without original sources have been removed. Only those matters that affect asset supply, pricing, distribution, or tool selection are retained.
