Aster removes points attributed to concentrated trading activity

Event: Aster removed points generated by concentrated XAUUSD1 trading from fewer than ten accounts over ten hours on September 7. The change cut total Epoch 2 points by about 50%. Epoch 1 and other points were unaffected.

Mechanism: After settlement, rewards are removed based on account concentration, and nominal trading volume is no longer directly equated with effective contributions.

Second-order effects: Trading incentives are shifting from simply chasing volume to quality audits. However, the rules regarding account associations, complaints, and early warnings remain undisclosed.

ScopeThe adjustment only affects a ten-hour window and the XAUUSD1 market. However, it reduces the number of points by about half, indicating that reward distribution is highly sensitive to the actions of a few accounts.

Charter offers the legal structure for token issuance as a shared service

The Charter Foundation was established by the Ink Foundation, GSR, and several law firms and accounting firms. It creates separate Cayman entities for each project. After successful issuance, these entities will become independent foundations.

The team estimates that the traditional three-entity architecture could cost over $100,000 before the TGE. Sharing templates could reduce upfront costs by more than half. However, these figures are based on service providers’ self-reported data, and there are no actual project examples.

ImplementationThe organization and partners have been made public. The reduction in costs is still estimated by the planters. It is necessary to verify the cycle, costs, and independent governance after the first batch of issuance is completed.

LAPTOP links 30% of token supply to real-world events

Hunter Biden’s LAPTOP token ties 30% of supply to 30 real-world events. The corresponding tokens are burned if an event occurs and donated if it does not. A further 20% is for airdrops and 30% goes to the founder.

In the first hour after the tokens went live, their market value reached approximately 110 billion USD. However, it dropped by more than 99%. This shows that complex allocation, locking up positions, and public narrative cannot replace initial liquidity and price discovery.

Measurement limits110 billion USD represents the instantaneous complete dilution valuation under extremely thin liquidity conditions. It does not represent the amount of cash that can be withdrawn. This metric is more suitable for observing startup distortions.
