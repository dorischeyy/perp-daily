November 17: CFTC sets out asset, clearing and weekend risk questions for round-the-clock derivatives trading

The CFTC’s December 15 roundtable agenda sets out three questions about round-the-clock derivatives. Which assets suit continuous trading? How would clearing capital, margin and tokenized collateral work? How would venues handle weekend liquidity, price discovery, circuit breakers, staffing and systems maintenance? The agenda sets up a regulatory discussion and grants no new product approvals.

November 17: CFTC pauses Kalshi contracts on words the president will say amid manipulation review

Kalshi has removed contracts that involve predicting what the president will say, as required by the CFTC. Regulators have suspended related self-certifications and initiated procedures, stating that the platform has not proven that such contracts are not susceptible to manipulation. Kalshi claims it has set up monitoring systems and opposes the decision, but further investigation is still needed. Whether these event-based contracts can be listed depends on evidence of anti-manipulation measures for each contract.
