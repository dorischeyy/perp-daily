The U.S. Treasury proposed rules under Section 3 of the GENIUS Act and opened a 60-day comment period. ImplementationThe rule is still in the proposal and comment stage. Final wording may change, but issuance, sales, and overseas compliance have emerged as explicit conditions for distribution.
