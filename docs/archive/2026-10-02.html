Hyperliquid policy group urges the EU to regulate perpetuals as derivatives outside MiCA

Event: The Hyperliquid Policy Center submitted comments to the European Commission’s MiCA review. It argued that perpetuals should be classified as MiFID II derivatives according to their economic substance, regardless of whether their records are on a public blockchain.

Mechanism: The submission calls for classification instrument by instrument and distinguishes order book perpetuals from CFDs where the platform is the counterparty. It also recommends advance disclosure of funding formulas, maintenance margin and liquidation thresholds.

ImplementationThis is merely an advisory opinion, not EU regulations. It remains uncertain whether the committee will adopt this approach and how it will be applied to on-chain frontends and market creators.

Coinbase International moves perpetual execution and funding to Deribit

Event: Coinbase International originally handled customer relationships and collateral management for perpetual contracts. The underlying transactions, positions, and executions were transferred to Deribit. Mechanism: Positions will be re-established after settlement at the reference price. The API will switch from Coinbase to Deribit JSON-RPC. Funding payments will be made continuously, starting at 08:00 UTC every day. A zero-fee range and asset limits will also be introduced.
