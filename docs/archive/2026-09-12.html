Phoenix adds TWAP execution for splitting large orders

Event: Phoenix introduced TWAP orders, allowing traders to split their target positions into multiple executions over time.

Mechanism: The system trades transactions in a predetermined cycle, reducing the impact of large orders that sweep through the order book. However, the average transaction price still depends on the slicing frequency, market depth, and price movement during the period.

Second-order effects: Execution tools are now expanding from market prices and limits to algorithmic splitting, which can improve the user experience for large transactions. Aster bases real-world asset rewards on average open interest throughout the day

Event: Aster announced that starting from Epoch 3, the USD1 RWA Boost will be calculated on a daily basis using UTC timekeeping, with a 24-hour average OI serving as the multiplier.

Mechanism: Rewards now use average open interest throughout the day to measure sustained positions. Replacing a single hourly snapshot reduces the scoring benefit of briefly adding positions just before settlement.

Second-order effects: Incentives shift from immediate transaction volumes to sustained positions. However, if account concentration, sampling anomalies, and self-transactions remain opaque, the average OI may be exploited by strategies that take advantage of capital.

ImplementationThe new metric will take effect starting from Epoch 3 on September 14. Sampling, abnormal account identification, and appeal rules have not yet been disclosed.

Uniswap directs stablecoin arbitrage spreads to liquidity providers

Uniswap has launched a StablePair Hook in the Ethereum USDC/USDG and USDC/USDT pools. When the price is within the reference range, a fixed spread is maintained. The higher the deviation from the reference range, the lower the transaction fees for corrections.

When arbitgers bring the pool price back to the reference price, fees decrease from high to low levels in each block. The price difference that was originally obtained by the fastest arbitgers can be more effectively returned to LP. The parameters can still be upgraded through governance improvements.

ScaleUniswap’s stablecoin exchange volume in the second quarter was approximately 43.4 billion USD, which is higher than the combined volume of the previous three venues. The new Hook currently covers only two Ethereum pools.
