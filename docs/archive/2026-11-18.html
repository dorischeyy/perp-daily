November 17: GRVT launches seven commodity perpetuals with funding based on traditional-market hedging costs

GRVT listed Stable Funding Perps on gold, silver, platinum, palladium, WTI, Brent and natural gas. They settle in USDC, and market makers quote funding based on the cost of hedging through traditional CFDs. Trading follows the underlying market’s hours, but positions can still be liquidated while it is closed.

Account security and trading distribution

November 17th, Lighter reopened the coin withdrawal process and tightened the API signature and public pool transfer permissions.

Lighter suspended the withdrawal of funds after a trader used non-official signature software, resulting in the reuse of nonces and the possibility of deriving the API private key. The security committee returned approximately 5 million USDC and reopened the service. The protocol subsequently requires that transactions to the public pool must be signed with a wallet, and the API signature must be kept confidential. Officials stated that the contract and official SDK were not affected, but isolating account permissions from keys has become a practical measure to prevent losses.

November 17: Backtrade launches HyperEVM perpetuals with a pool funded by traders’ losses

Backtrade’s new HyperEVM product funds its USDC pool with traders’ losses. Outside liquidity providers cannot deposit. The unaudited product allows up to 1,000x leverage, and the ability to withdraw gains depends on the pool’s balance.
