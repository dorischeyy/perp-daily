3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: Trading volume recovery is no longer considered evidence of capacity improvement. Capacity is adjusted only when there is a combined improvement in continuous OI and trading volume, asset-level order books, and exit impacts.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: The product team can prioritize reviewing liquidity based on rolling turnover, depth of quotes, and exit impacts. Risk control parameters are not adjusted based on daily trading volume.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If OI continues to rise for multiple days, the ratio of OI to transactions stabilizes, and asset depth increases, with exit shocks below the current threshold, then the conclusion of not holding any positions for a long time can be overturned.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: Expired updates for AgentOS, OpenAI, and Microsoft products have been deleted. There are also no activities related to the listing of new coins, price fluctuations, or lack of verifiable release dates.

1. **Top traders or whales**: The aggregation of BTC at the exchange level cannot prove the depth of asset-level exits. The text describes the trading rebound as a signal of turnover, rather than a permanent capacity expansion.
2. **Responsible person for competing products.**: Submissions to GMGN and LaunchFair can prove that tools and configurations are up and running, but they cannot prove user adoption or transaction conversion. The text retains the boundaries.
3. **CEO or strategy-related issues.**: Approval for OCC conditions and bank cooperation are regulated, but one bank hasn’t started operations yet, and another is planned to launch in 2027. These cannot be counted as revenue before they’re operational.
4. **Skeptics**: OpenRouter’s acquisition was reported by media outlets, and Stripe did not comment. Both the headline and the text remain consistent with the reported information.
5. **Editor**: Perp DEX covers three aspects: overall turnover, Hyperliquid single venues, and cross-venue verification between Gate and Bybit. No conclusions from yesterday were repeated.
6. **Newcomers**: The text explains OI and transaction ratios, conditional approvals, non-variable costs, and watermark boundaries. Readers are not required to understand these mechanisms on their own.
