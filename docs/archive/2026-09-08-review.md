# Editorial review

Date: 2026-09-08 · Issue 71

## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: Lighter has provided the first attributable third-party distribution volume. The BNB Chain launch platform begins competing for asset pairing, while Harmony shifts the focus of security and maintenance issues to the entire L1 exit strategy.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: The competitive variables in Perp DEX are expanding from direct website transactions and the number of deals to distribution entry points, cross-platform specifications, and verifiable lifecycle states.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: The focus should shift from observing the number of new markets to verifying whether third-party distribution leads to real increases in volume. It’s also important to ensure that market specifications and conditions can be uniformly managed.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: On the product side, priority should be given to verifying channel attribution, contract specifications, and final controls. Only then can decisions be made regarding allocating resources to more complex targets and external distribution channels.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If distribution entry points do not add new users or retain them, and if cross-venue specifications do not lead to failed transactions, or if there are no accidents during lifecycle transitions, then related priorities should be downgraded.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: The section that listed only two new contract codes and leverage ratios from Binance has been removed. It wasn’t included in the Perp DEX section to avoid filling in empty spaces.
