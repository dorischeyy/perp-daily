Volume rebounds at four major perpetual venues as open interest falls

Event: On August 25, combined 24-hour volume at Binance, Hyperliquid, Bybit and Gate increased 51.6%, from about 1,069,900 BTC to 1,622,400 BTC. Open interest fell 2.2%, from roughly 883,200 BTC to 863,700 BTC.

Measurement limitsThe BTC-denominated series covers four venues. Its movement does not establish deleveraging across the entire market.

Hyperliquid policy advocates call for classifying perpetuals by economic structure

Event: The Hyperliquid Policy Center urged the SEC and CFTC to classify perpetuals first by economic structure, then apply rules for equities, commodities or cryptoassets. It proposed joint oversight of security futures and notice registration as a route across venues.

Mechanism: The proposed framework starts with contract risk and settlement structure, reducing the chance that otherwise similar perpetuals receive entirely different treatment solely because of their venue or underlying asset.

Second-order effects: If regulators adopt this approach, expanding RWA, equity and commodity perpetual listings will depend more on contract classification, customer protections and coordination between agencies, alongside the tradability of each underlying.

ScopeThe position comes from an industry policy organization. It is not an SEC or CFTC rule or a court ruling. The HIP-3 volume and open-interest figures also come from that organization.

OKX adds expiring X-Perps for ASTER and YB

Event: OKX announced ASTERUSD and YBUSD X-Perp listings for August 24 on the web, app and API. It expressly labeled the products Expiry Perps.

Mechanism: The structure brings token exposure into a common derivatives framework with an expiry feature. The announcement supplied opening times but no expiry dates, indexes, funding terms or final settlement details.

Second-order effects: Maturity design is expanding under the perpetual label. Interfaces need to explain contract term, settlement status and risk parameters, since the label alone does not establish that a product runs indefinitely.

ScopeThe official announcement gives the product name and opening time. It does not specify expiry or settlement parameters. The subsequent contract specifications are needed to establish those mechanics.
