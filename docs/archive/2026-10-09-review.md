### Six reader perspectives

1. Top traders: Both OKX’s listing and Binance’s margin qualifications lack real-depth, discounts, and extreme market performance. The text does not describe the available entry points as truly liquid.
2. Competitor product leaders: Bitwise’s total volume, Securitize’s rights structure, and Aspecta’s settlement window correspond to scale, asset rights, and lifecycle control, respectively. These three do not merge into a single competitive conclusion.
3. CEO Strategy: Samsung and Upbit have indicated that the distribution channels will continue to shift to mobile systems and local fiat currency exchanges. However, these channels do not automatically result in protocol retention.
4. Risk management compliance: ESMA requires coverage of all aspects of trading, transfers, custody, and advisory services. Binance and Securitize’s regional and compliance boundaries remain intact.
5. Skeptics: Bitwise data comes from a single research report; Google’s capabilities are based on published summaries; Coinbase’s metrics are based on proprietary benchmarks. All these factors indicate that certain conclusions cannot be generalized.
6. New projects: The text distinguishes between projects that are already in use, those that are planned to be launched, regulatory opinions, third-quarter statistics, and manufacturer evaluations. The status of these projects is not mixed together.
