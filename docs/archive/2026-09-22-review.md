# Editorial review for issue 84

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: OKX has changed the margin requirements for hedging accounts from a bilateral sum to taking the higher side. Coinbase has embedded IPO allocation into its retail app. ECB has allowed tokenized assets to use central bank currency for settlement. Trading competition is now advancing towards account efficiency, primary distribution, and settlement at the underlying level.

**What is the most important finding?**: Binance uses its own order book EWMA to complement the price index for perpetual foreign exchange trading on weekends. This clearly shows that trading throughout the day does not mean that the underlying market provides external quotes throughout the day.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: TradFi perpetual contracts are no longer just expanding their targets. They are also being transformed to include weekend pricing and hedging margins. The Launchpad has also expanded from token subscriptions to licensed IPO offerings and issuance price protection.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating perpetual derivatives or early-stage assets, it is important to separate factors such as reference prices, margin deductions, rights attributes, allocation rules, and abnormal settlements. It is not enough to simply look at the quantity of assets or the “24/7 trading” label.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If the API and the page remain inconsistent for a long time after OKX’s grayscale transition, and if Binance’s weekend index shows uncontrollable deviations, and if Coinbase’s IPO fails to achieve real allocation over the long term, then without further expansion of institutional trading and services by Pontes, it is necessary to downgrade our judgments regarding account efficiency, distribution, and settlement at the underlying level.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Excluded OKX's routine equity listings that day, a single Bybit equity perpetual, another batch of MEXC equity contracts, OpenAI Academy and Google's education program. The retained items change a calculation method, price source, rights boundary or verification mechanism.
