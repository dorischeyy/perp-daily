### Six reader perspectives

1. **Top traders/major players**: Using stock tokens as collateral can increase asset efficiency, but details such as the collateral discount, liquidation parameters, and liquidity have not been disclosed. The price at which Ostium reopens will directly determine the profit or loss during the freeze period.
2. **Responsible person for competing products.**: The changes at Lighter do not involve adding another market, but rather turning wallet assets into trading margin. The changes at pump.fun only support migration within five minutes after the transition, which does not mean a long-term improvement.
3. **CEO/Strategy**: Wallet distribution competition is moving towards collateral reuse. However, the more assets there are, the heavier the risk model and the burden on the jurisdiction.
4. **Skeptics**: Lighter does not provide any data. The 20% increase in pump.fun’s performance and the estimated annual losses are all based on platform-specific data. The statements in the text do not indicate that they are verified results.
5. **Editor**: The two Perp DEX entries address issues related to capital efficiency and accident recovery. The Launchpad entries do not enforce any mandatory associations with products, nor do they duplicate the settlement assets and CME content from July 21.
6. **Newcomers**: The text explains why stock tokens are not the stocks themselves, why asset isolation still poses a risk of freezing, and at which stage the buyback process of BOOST takes place.

- Narrative consistency: Lighter and Ostium each develop two separate storylines. HIP-3 has expired, but no fake progress has been created.
- Readability: 8. Avoid using technical terms to describe collateral reuse, freezing, and migration of buyers.
- House View 9: Implementing features does not equal adoption, and isolating assets does not mean risk is eliminated.
- Clarity level 9: The Crypto and AI sections were omitted, leaving only information that can influence judgment.

### Top three revisions

1. The preliminary draft tends to describe Lighter as an ordinary wallet integration. It has been changed to upgrade stock tokens from trading assets to perpetual margin, and to include restrictions on issuers, price sources, and jurisdiction.
2. The draft only repeated the amount stolen by Ostium. It has been revised to describe how isolated contracts, frozen positions, and reopening pricing contribute to risks in the second phase.
3. The draft relies on pump.fun’s metrics for liquidity improvement. It has been changed to platform estimates, and it is clarified that five-minute support does not prove long-term stability.
