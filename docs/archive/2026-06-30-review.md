The path to institutionalization is more difficult than it seems. This has implications for the business model of on-chain platforms, which should be discussed more thoroughly in the inspiration section.
- This week’s narrative focus is: “Decentralized perp faces regulation,” “Institutions don’t buy into it,” and “Aster uses real funds to compete in terms of trading volume.” These three vectors are aligned, forming a major theme: “By 2026, perp DEXs will compete in terms of operational efficiency and compliance.” This can be further clarified in the lead section.
