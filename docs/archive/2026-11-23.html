Ostium includes market hours and order book depth in price reports

Ostium’s real-world asset price reports include trading sessions, holidays and bid and ask depth. While a market is closed, traders can place limit or stop orders, but cannot submit market orders. November 22: OpenAI proposes a shared slowdown range for frontier model training based on log perplexity

OpenAI disclosed that the logarithmic perplexity of GPT-3.5, GPT-4, and the latest models in its internal code dataset is approximately 0.99, 0.91, and 0.76, respectively. It was proposed that laboratories should limit progress around the range of 0.68 to 0.70 before reaching common monitoring milestones. This metric can be used to track training progress across different scales, but direct comparisons between datasets are not possible.
