## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Binance and OKX launched SOXX perpetual contracts on the same day. However, they adopted opposite rules for settlement frequencies after funding fees reached their limits. This results in different cost paths for the same asset.
**What is the most important finding?**: Binance continues to maintain an eight-hour settlement process even when the fees reach the upper or lower limits. OKX will automatically switch to hourly settlement. When comparing perpetual contracts with RWA, it’s important not to focus solely on the underlying asset and leverage.
**Which prior assessment changed?**: Differences among perpetuals on real-world assets now extend beyond the source of funding and trading hours to the way venues settle funding on the same underlying at extreme rates.
**What decision can readers make?**: In product evaluation, it is important to consider factors such as the calculation basis for funding fees, the order of scaling, the frequency of settlement transitions, and the schedule for market closures as separate parameters. It is not sufficient to simply compare the names of contracts alone.
**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: Recalculate holding costs if the first ten SOXX funding settlements after launch show the same frequency at both venues, or if both venues depart from their announced schedules.
**Which news recap was removed?**: The “predict.fun” platform has been deleted, with no clear timestamp information available. Variational platform has been removed, along with its aggregate page and general expansion candidates.
