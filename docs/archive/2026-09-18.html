CFTC narrows broker registration requirements for passive trading software

Event: The CFTC’s Market Participants Division extended its no-action position for passive software providers to all providers that meet its conditions. Qualifying software may connect users to registered futures commission merchants, introducing brokers and designated contract markets without the division recommending enforcement for failure to register as an introducing broker solely on that basis. This clarifies provider responsibilities in the trading infrastructure story tracked since August 25.

Mechanism: Regulators are beginning to define the boundaries between software that actually performs brokering and trading functions. This leaves room for regulated derivatives in wallets and front-end connections. However, this remains a conditional position for staff members; it does not mean that software providers are exempt from other registration, marketing, or behavioral obligations.

ImplementationThis is a conditional position of inaction. It is not an official rule of the committee, and it does not cover unregistered trading venues.
