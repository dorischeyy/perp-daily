### Six reader perspectives

1. **Top traders/major players**: The launch of SPCXUSD1 does not mean that the USD1 trading platform already has sufficient depth. The performance of OI, price spreads, and liquidation on the first day was not disclosed. CME’s 1 billion USD in trading volume was accumulated over six weekends, involving both futures and options. This cannot be compared to the daily perpetual trading volume.
2. **Responsible person for competing products.**: Binance’s real change is turning settlement assets into distribution tools, rather than adding another stock target. CME indicates that 24/7 availability is no longer a selling point exclusive to crypto-native products.
3. **CEO/Strategy**: Today’s main issue is that trading entrances are competing for targets, settlement currencies, and trading times. If only one criterion can be considered, then it should be verified whether the new settlement mechanism can handle independent order flows, rather than simply replicating the market.
4. **Skeptics**: The CLARITY Act’s ethical provisions come from industry sources. The official text has not been released yet. The text does not describe political agreements as regulatory rules that have been implemented.
5. **Editor**: Two perpdex articles discuss how settlement assets can compete and how continuous trading can be commodified. There is no repetition of the unified matchmaking between Coinbase and Deribit from yesterday. Predictions regarding market OI and ETH funds news were dismissed due to insufficient relevance.
6. **Newcomers**: The text explains the difference between direct settlement and auxiliary collateral. It also explains why CME’s maturity contracts are different from perpetual contracts that depend on the funding rate.
