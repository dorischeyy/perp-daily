November 9: OKX details how it will handle perpetual positions and orders through the SNDK stock split

For SNDK’s 8-for-1 split, OKX will pause related spot, perpetual and X-Perp trading on November 20. It will settle old positions at the pre-adjustment mark price and reopen them at the adjusted mark price with eight times the quantity. Untriggered take-profit and stop-loss orders will be canceled. Unfilled remainders of partially filled orders will receive new IDs. Grid bots must be restarted manually.

Perpetual market design

Avantis offers loss rebates on counterbalancing positions to reduce LPs’ one-sided exposure

Avantis’s USDC vault is the counterparty to every trade. Once long-short skew exceeds 55%, a new position on the smaller side can lock in a loss rebate of 0% to 20%. The rebate does not reset if the market later rebalances. LPs receive trading fees, and the protocol buffer pays trader profits first. Rebates encourage positions that reduce the vault’s directional exposure.
