Backpack enables actual shares to serve as perpetual collateral

Backpack listed four perpetual markets for round-the-clock trading, covering MU, SNDK, SPY and QQQ. Users can also put actual Micron and SanDisk shares held on the platform toward cross-asset portfolio margin.

Stocks are still held by licensed brokerage firms. The four perpetual contracts only offer price exposure; they do not come with shareholder rights or dividends.

ScopeIn the initial phase, only Micron and SanDisk stocks can be used as collateral. The regions where products can be used and the risk parameters are still subject to platform restrictions.

Ondo submits three market structure comment letters to the SEC and CFTC

Ondo has submitted three letters of opinion to the SEC and CFTC regarding perpetual futures, portfolio margining, and on-chain market data. They argue that funding payments and daily market monitoring can fulfill the functions of traditional maturity convergence mechanisms.

The proposal also requires that margin rules be based on real-time risk assessment, and regulators are allowed to directly use shared, verifiable on-chain records.

ImplementationThe three letters refer to industry policy recommendations, not rules adopted by the SEC or CFTC.

New Jersey petitioned the U.S. Supreme Court to review a Third Circuit ruling that federal law preempts state regulation of sports contracts on prediction markets.

Circle enables native cross-chain EURC transfers through CCTP

Beeline integrates MCP natively into workforce management

Beeline has integrated MCP into its supplier management system. Approved agents can access source data, employment information, and compliance data based on their existing identities, roles, and approval levels.
