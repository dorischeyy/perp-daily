Perpetual market design

Ostium queues real-world asset orders while markets are closed and fetches prices at execution

Ostium does not accept market orders for real-world assets while the underlying market is closed. Limit and stop orders can queue for execution after reopening. At execution, Stork-operated nodes generate an onchain price report with the market session, bid-ask spread and holiday status. Full closes carry no fee. A partial close incurs a $0.10 oracle fee.
