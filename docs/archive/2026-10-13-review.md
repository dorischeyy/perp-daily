## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: TradFi perpetuals bring ETF path dependence into round-the-clock trading. On the issuance side, investors can accept a lockup in exchange for a valuation discount. Both affect risk assessment.

**What is the most important finding?**: Binance is launching ETFs, leveraged ETFs, and perpetual tokens. This indicates that the competition in the RWA perpetual market has evolved from expanding listings to pricing and margin management for complex underlying assets.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The TradFi perpetuals story shifts from bulk listings to risks embedded in ETFs. Institutional stablecoin adoption now includes a potential collateral-mirroring arrangement alongside custody.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating TradFi perpetuals, verify the underlying market’s closing hours, ETF rebalancing, index sources and depth for large orders before deciding on listings and margin parameters.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If the four new contracts do not result in any transactions or Open Interests over a long period, or if the price continues to deviate from the spot market price during market holidays and fails to converge, then the judgment of asset category expansion needs to be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Information regarding ordinary asset listings, self-reported growth of projects, duplicate market size rankings, and financing news with no changes in mechanisms has been removed.

6. For new users, the text explains the path dependence of ETFs, the status of Launchpad, margin trading, and the release process. It avoids confusing the actual launch, planning, testing, and adoption with the actual implementation.
