# Perp DEX Daily editorial review · 2026-10-25

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Twenty listed companies opposed the establishment of tokenized stock venues on OKX and ICE. This indicates that the main constraints for RWA trading have shifted from whether the platform can be launched to issues related to issuer acceptance, shareholder rights, and unified price discovery.

**What is the most important finding?**: Tokenized stock platforms have received regulatory exemptions, but they still face opposition from issuers. NASDAQ warns that off-exchange liquidity may be weakened, which could lead to inconsistent pricing. This is more important than adding just ten new equity perpetual futures contracts, as it directly limits the supply of tradable assets. However, the final platform rules and actual exit lists are still undecided.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The previous assessment focused on weekend depth and pricing while underlying markets were closed. Scaling real-world asset perpetuals also requires checking issuer consent, the rights holders receive, and whether venues participate in consolidated market data.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating equity perpetual futures or tokenized stocks, it is necessary to verify issuer permissions, rights mapping, source of pricing during market closures, and the actual order book. Only then can a decision be made regarding the prioritization of assets. The number of assets involved should not be used as evidence.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If a large number of issuers withdraw their objections, and if the venue provides unified quotes and market data, and if stable trading and open interest continue to be observed after the launch, then the conclusion that “asset supply is constrained by rights and price discovery” needs to be re-evaluated.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Deleted announcements about stablecoin creation without an official publication date, redundant reports about Kimi updates, and single-platform announcements that only list new items without specifying rights and depth gaps.
