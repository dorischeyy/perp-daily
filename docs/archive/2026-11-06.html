November 5: Bitget uses asset mirroring to connect external custody to spot, margin and futures trading

Bitget launched the Open Custody Network. Asset mirroring lets eligible customers retain assets at participating banks or institutional custodians while using Bitget’s spot, margin and futures markets. Custody and execution are separate. Bitget did not identify the first custodians or disclose how mirrored balances settle, how margin calls work or how long exceptional withdrawals take.

November 5: Bitget says small positions on 21 FX CFDs can use up to 2,000x leverage

For 21 FX CFDs, Bitget cut the margin requirement to 0.05% on the first 50,000 USDT of position notional, permitting up to 2,000x leverage on that portion. Smaller positions use less capital, but both size and events affect the applicable limits.
