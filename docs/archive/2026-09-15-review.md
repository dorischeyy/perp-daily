# Editorial review for issue 78

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Reya tied migration to user access. SGX provided foreign perpetual entry points for American institutions. The Robinhood Chain revealed that users of the brokerage app and those participating in native activities on the chain are not the same group of people.

**What is the most important finding?**: According to CoinDesk Research, users of the Robinhood main app contribute only 1% to 2% of transactions on this chain. This indicates that brand entry points, total volume on the chain, and actual user migration must be evaluated separately.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: Existing users can be directly transferred to the blockchain through brokers. However, it’s necessary to separately evaluate blockchain-native customer acquisition, promotional traffic, and main app users.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating distribution partnerships, priority should be given to verifying channel sources, initial deposits, initial transactions, retention after subsidies, and fee recovery. The total transactions of the partnership should not be used as a substitute for user conversion.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If subsequent data proves that the number of Robinhood main app users increases significantly during transactions on the blockchain, and they continue to remain active even after subsidies end, or if there are significant failures and losses during the migration to Reya, then this assessment may need to be adjusted.

**Which news recap was removed?**: The duplicate versions of Phoenix TWAP, Aster’s funding payments, HYPE price versions, announcements about Parabola’s launch on future dates, and ordinary coin listings without any rules changes have been deleted.

### Six reader perspectives

1. Top traders/big whales: Reya only mentioned access migration, without specifying a launch date, market-making incentives, or rules for disposing of positions in version 1. GRVT also did not disclose mortgage discounts, loss deductions, or exit rules.
2. Product manager for competing products: ApeX combines direct payment with competition to create a customer funnel. However, the 6,000 USDT activity order flow cannot be considered a natural demand in the political market.
3. CEO/Strategy: The Robinhood Chain explains that brokerage products can aggregate supply on the blockchain. However, the estimated proportion of transactions conducted through the main app is only 1% to 2%. The scale of cooperation does not equal the conversion of native users.
4. Skeptics: 98.4% of Meteora and pump.fun’s activities involve MemeFees that have already been integrated into the platform. Direct pool creation and those not integrated into the platform are not included in the denominator.
5. Editor-in-Chief: Perp DEX covers three areas: matchmaking migration, collateral, and deposit distribution. Crypto covers three areas: market access, legislation, and data infrastructure. There are no price versions retained.
6. For new users, order book migration, dual-stablecoin margin requirements, graduation metrics, and AI behavior guidelines all help to define mechanisms and boundaries. The parameters that are not disclosed by the source are not guessed.
