# Editorial review for issue 83

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Coinbase has moved from mere registration intentions for single-stock perpetual contracts in the United States to formal product rule declarations. PancakeSwap has integrated wallet points and pre-IPO private placements into the same distribution portal. Competition in trading products continues to focus on compliance structures and qualification levels.

**What is the most important finding?**: The Coinbase document also reveals two thresholds: registration with the SEC and product approval with the CFTC. It is important to distinguish between filing, approval, and launch, rather than mistaking regulatory documents for actual products.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The U.S. regulatory framework for perpetual contracts has evolved from only physical registration and cooperation schemes to specific rules for individual perpetual contracts. Launchpad’s approach has also shifted from token issuance to private placement with qualified tiers.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating RWA or perpetual products, it is necessary to first verify four aspects: rights, regulation, settlement, and real adoption. Only then can a decision be made regarding integration or investment in resources. It is not enough to judge based solely on the product’s name and announcement status.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If Coinbase’s application is withdrawn or not approved by the CFTC, and if PancakeSwap’s first activities fail to involve actual subscription and settlement, and if NEAR Intents’ locked positions and transactions decline significantly in subsequent snapshots, then it is necessary to downgrade our judgments regarding compliance improvements and distribution expansion.

**Which news recap was removed?**: The following assets have been removed from the list: Bitunix’s equity perpetual futures, Bybit’s general expansion, OpenAI’s legal products, and Kalshi’s old financing data. Only those entries that can update rules, mechanisms, rights, or actual quantities are retained.
