Trade.xyz offers compensation after an anomalous external SK Hynix trade affects multiple price feeds

Event: On July 29, Trade.xyz said it would cover eligible SKHYNIX perpetual liquidation losses. Several data providers had relayed the same external trade, and the mark price fell from $1,127.90 to $917.25, nearly 19%. This was the thirteenth development tracked since June 12.

Mechanism: The oracle converted Korean pre-market trades from won to dollars under its published specification. Multiple data suppliers relayed one anomalous trade into the mark used for margin and liquidation. Trade.xyz was considering giving its own order-book prices more weight.

Second-order effects: Multiple vendors can report the same underlying trade, so vendor count alone does not establish price-source independence. The episode exposed the need for clear rules on outlier detection, liquidation pauses and how losses are allocated afterward.

ScopeAt the time of reporting, the contract had traded more than $1.5 billion in 24 hours, with open interest near $600 million. The number of traders compensated and the total amount remained undisclosed.

Binance offers gold and silver options at its ADGM-regulated venue, with retail clients restricted to buying options

On July 29, Binance announced USDT-settled gold and silver options on ADGM-regulated Nest Exchange. Retail clients may buy options. Qualified institutions and liquidity providers may also sell them. The contracts do not involve physical delivery.

Nest already offered gold and silver perpetuals. Limiting retail customers to purchasing options caps their maximum loss at the premium, while qualified participants take on writing and market-making risk. This expands commodity exposure from linear leveraged positions to options with permissions tailored to the participant.

ImplementationThe initial offering covers gold and silver. The announcement did not provide an opening time, expiry structure, volume, open interest, or quoted depth. It establishes the product and access framework.
