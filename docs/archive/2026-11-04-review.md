## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: The CFTC has explicitly approved US regulated single-share perpetual contracts for the first time. On the same day, Kalshi transformed regulatory approval into four tradable stock contracts. This issue also involves responsibilities related to splitting the primary market, HIP-3 market, and order book migration.

**What is the most important finding?**: The U.S. single perpetual contract has progressed from plans with ICE and Trade[XYZ] to actual trading with Kalshi. However, stock contracts are still subject to specific time constraints. This fact directly affects the competitive analysis of RWA perpetual contracts. Additionally, it preserves liquidity and trading time boundaries, making it a priority over regular expansion options.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: Regulated equity perpetuals have progressed from approval and planned clearing arrangements to live products. Regulatory authorization has not automatically delivered crypto-style 24/7 trading or consolidated liquidity.

**What decision can readers make?**: Evaluate regulatory eligibility, actual trading hours, the oracle provider, settlement authority during halts and executable depth separately for equity perpetuals. Launch status alone does not establish those conditions.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: Reconsider the view that trading hours are the main constraint if Kalshi's equity contracts fail to sustain trading, or if venues show that single-stock perpetuals maintain stable depth, consistent pricing, and continuous exits while cash markets are closed.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Candidates that only include plans for spot trading at Moscow Exchange, expansion of standard contracts, and model promotions have been removed. They cannot be updated or evaluated for risks.
