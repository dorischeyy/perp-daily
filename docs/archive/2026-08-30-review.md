## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: Bybit introduces 24/7 options, portfolio strategies, and margin trading to equity perpetual futures. This means that product competition has evolved from simply expanding the list of tradable products to a comprehensive derivatives stack.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: With the addition of options to equity perpetual futures, risks are no longer determined solely by direction, leverage, and funding payments. Other factors such as expiration times, Greeks, volatility surfaces, and margin requirements also become important.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: We are shifting the comparison of equity perpetual futures based on the number of assets, leverage, and fees. Instead, we now consider options expiration times, volatility surfaces, and margin requirements as key aspects of product competitiveness.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: The product side will not immediately follow the trend of expanding options products. First, we need to verify the depth of the existing equity perpetual futures under market closures, gap trades, and continuous market-making conditions. Only after that can we decide whether to establish a separate option risk layer.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If there is no continuous trading and bilateral depth after the launch in September, or if there are significant accidents, losses, or price deviations in combined margins, the strategic priority of the options layer should be downgraded.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: We have removed any listings that only include equity perpetual futures codes, activity names, or single listing times. There are no new mechanisms, magnitudes, or verifiable boundaries for these candidates.
