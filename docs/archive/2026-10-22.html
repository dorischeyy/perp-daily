The rules set requirements for reserves, redemption, custody, and risk management for US and foreign issuers of stablecoins under OCC regulation. These rules will take effect on January 18, 2027. October 21: Solana mainnet switches to Alpenglow consensus

Alpenglow moves validator votes offchain and confirms blocks through certificates. It aims to cut finality from about six seconds to approximately 150 milliseconds. It activated at epoch 1064, without a change in how wallets or apps submit transactions. Mainnet measurements still need to validate the latency target. Removing onchain votes also reduces reported transaction counts.
