Event: KuCoin conducted an API upgrade lasting about 120 minutes on August 29. Spot, margin, futures, options and unified-account services had no general shutdown, but WebSocket connections could briefly disconnect, lose data or lag.

Mechanism: Trading service availability and data connection continuity are separate. A client may need to reconnect and rebuild its state after a disruption even while the venue itself remains open for trading.
