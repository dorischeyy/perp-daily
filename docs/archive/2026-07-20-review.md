### Six reader perspectives

1. **Top traders/major players**: The unified matching engine entering production does not mean that the client positions have been transferred. It also does not mean that more than 125 markets have achieved unified depth. The complete migration of nodes is scheduled for 09-09.
2. **Responsible person for competing products.**: Traditional channels are breaking down stablecoin collateral and funding rates into manageable components. What can be learned from this is cost visibility, not simply copying CFD packaging.
3. **CEO/Strategy**: The speed at which new perpetual contracts are introduced continues to increase. Today, the most important decision is to shift the focus from the quantity of assets to the efficiency of cross-asset funding and the quality of execution.
4. **Skeptics**: Perpetual CFD materials were provided by Pepperstone. The text does not acknowledge their market size and cost advantages. Only verifiable billing structures are retained, while the credibility score is reduced.
5. **Editor**: Two entries on perpdex discuss how infrastructure should be integrated and how regulatory channels should be translated into mechanisms. Uniswap only includes peripheral information; it does not explicitly mention fees associated with spot protocols as part of perp progress.
6. **Newcomers**: The text explains why the unified matching does not mean the migration is complete, how the initial margin is transferred through USDC, and why the v4 dynamic rates require a set of rules.
