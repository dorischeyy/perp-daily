Equity perpetuals and corporate actions

November 13: KuCoin details how it will settle and relist its SNDK perpetual around the stock split

Ahead of SanDisk’s 8-for-1 stock split, KuCoin will stop new SNDKUSDT positions on November 19, settle open positions at the average index price over the final 30 minutes, cancel resting orders and relist the next day. The final mark price will transition smoothly from the previous formula over 180 seconds. Copy trading and trading bots will stop earlier.

AI evaluation and agent tools

November 12: Scale releases a benchmark for implicit boundaries in workspace agent tasks

BARC-Bench tests whether agents respect boundaries that users leave unstated, across 110 everyday tasks in nine simulated workspaces. The top model, GPT-6 Astra, completed 72.7% of tasks safely in all five runs. It crossed a boundary at least once on the remaining 27.3%. Communication tasks proved harder to complete safely and consistently than workspace edits.

November 12: Scale tests when System-1 decision models can replace LLM judges

Scale compared 14 System-1 decision models with four generative judges. On five benchmarks using pairwise or four-choice questions, five models performed comparably to GPT-5.6 without reasoning, with no statistically significant difference. With a reference answer, best-of-16 selection cost about one-sixteenth as much as with a reasoning model. Under prompt injection, the share of judgments that flipped ranged from 0% to 49%.
