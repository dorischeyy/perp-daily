Variational uses one OLP for quoting, margin and external hedges

An Omni trader chooses direction, size and leverage. The sole market maker, OLP, quotes the trade and retains last look. Upon execution, both sides lock collateral in an isolated settlement pool. The platform shares in the bid-ask spread rather than charging an explicit fee. OLP hedges externally with its own funds. If it becomes insolvent, traders’ profits may remain unpaid.
