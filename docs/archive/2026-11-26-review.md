# Perp DEX Daily editorial review · November 26, 2026 · Issue 143

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: KuCoin described how contracts with different risk profiles could share an insurance fund and how two stages of auto-deleveraging (ADL) would work within a pool. Binance made five tokenized securities available for spot trading and portfolio margin. Both announcements expand the assets or markets that can share a risk buffer.

**What is the most important finding?**: A shared fund gives contracts a common buffer in extreme markets. Traders need to see where losses originated, which stage of ADL applies, and when the pool recovers. With tokenized securities, collateral eligibility alone says little about share ownership or whether a holder can exit at face value.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: Competition over margin has shifted from the list of supported tokens to the rights attached to securities certificates, their value after haircuts, and their use across markets. Comparing liquidation systems now requires checking which contracts share losses and which positions face ADL first.

**What decision can readers make?**: Product and risk teams can show the instrument's legal form and collateral haircut alongside its insurance pool, current ADL stage, and recovery status. A single balance or liquidation label obscures those differences.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: Withdraw the relevant finding if KuCoin cancels the shared fund or two-stage ADL in its final rules, or if Binance excludes these bStocks from the margin programs named in its notice.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Removed routine equity perpetual listings, brief AI service incidents, and old model news with no new release. The OKX item retains the four Hong Kong stocks, the change in funding frequency at the rate limit, and the questions about pricing outside cash-market hours.
