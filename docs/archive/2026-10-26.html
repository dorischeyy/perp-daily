Trading products and access

BULK supports multiple markets with unified portfolio margin and plans front-end commissions

Traders can offset risk across markets in one portfolio margin account and use up to 40x leverage. The system supports partial liquidation, and front ends share one deterministic order book. Transparent onchain builder commissions are planned but not live, so no current distribution revenue can be attributed to them.

Avantis rebates fees on losing trades while its vault bears payout and liquidity risk

Traders pay no fixed opening fee and no trading fee on a losing position. They give up a share of profits only when they win. Higher- and lower-risk vaults provide liquidity. Their returns depend on net fees and trader gains or losses. If the buffer ratio falls below 0.90, the exit fee can reach 2.5%.
