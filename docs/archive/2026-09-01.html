KuCoin makes 630 perpetual contracts available for copy trading

Event: KuCoin’s upgraded copy trading supports 630 USDT-margined perpetuals. Lead traders can run strategies on most contracts, and followers can automatically copy their positions.

Mechanism: Copy trading turns market selection, entry and exit timing, and position direction into signals that followers can replicate. The unified account distributes lead traders’ strategies across less widely traded contracts.

Second-order effects: Broader coverage gives followers access to less widely traded assets. It also makes concentration in individual lead traders, slippage, capacity constraints and simultaneous exits more significant. KuCoin disclosed neither copy-trading limits per contract nor actual uptake.
