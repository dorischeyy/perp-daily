## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: The trading volume yesterday has decreased, but the existing positions have not been withdrawn simultaneously. Additionally, the CFTC and the State of New York have issued regulatory requirements that are opposite to those of Kalshi. This has shifted market risks from expansion to ongoing capacity and execution conflicts.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: When the combined transaction volume of the three major perpetual markets decreases by approximately 10.6%, OI still increases by about 0.4%. This indicates that a decrease in transaction volume does not mean that risk associated with positions disappears simultaneously.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: Continuous abnormal data needs to be isolated. The decline in transaction volume in normal venues must be judged together with OI retention, asset-level depth, and pool utilization. Capacity adjustments cannot be triggered independently.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: Risk management resources should be prioritized for monitoring asset-level exits and OI retention. Prediction-based products should not be expanded until federal and state conflicts are resolved.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If OI continues to decline over several days, or if asset-level evidence shows that the ability to exit becomes more effective, then a higher automatic risk reduction weight can be assigned to transaction volume decreases.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: News about ordinary coin prices, eToro financial reports, MoneyGram’s integration into the blockchain, and news about product expansion without any new mechanisms have been deleted.
