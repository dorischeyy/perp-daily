Perpspad links token launches to perpetual positions

On Solana, Perpspad assigns each newly launched token an onchain subwallet holding a leveraged perpetual position routed through Imperial and executed on Phoenix. Trading fees continually replenish the position.

ImplementationThe project has published its white paper and opened its launch interface. It has yet to publish graduation counts, position sizes or liquidation records.

Binance uses reinvestment to distribute dividends on four bStocks

Binance reinvests net cash dividends from Broadcom, QQQ, Meta and Seagate in the corresponding bStock after deducting taxes and fees. Onchain holders receive the economic benefit through an adjustment to the multiplier.

bStocks are certificates of underlying securities, not direct ownership. Trading continues during the dividend distribution period, but conversions, rechargements, and withdrawals are suspended for the underlying assets.

ScopeThis announcement covers four products. No details about the net dividend rate, deductions, or actual distribution completion time were disclosed.
