## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Reya has migrated the order book to interfaces and dates. Arc is entering the public mainnet through its ecosystem. UK regulators also begin accepting new applications under the new system.

**What is the most important finding?**: Reya is not just announcing v2; it requires programmatic integration for migration, REST, signatures, and WebSocket. The transition to a new architecture has already begun with external system modifications.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: Pons’ distribution on the Robinhood Chain no longer focuses solely on the total transaction fees. Comprehensive daily data shows that approximately 84% of the funds go to the supply side. It’s necessary to separate the costs associated with supply, protocol income, and the quality of transactions.

**What decision can readers make?**: Before deciding whether to launch a trading product, it is important to verify interface compatibility, position handling, rollback plans, and time frames. The allocation of distribution resources also depends on transaction volume, cost allocation, and the quality of graduation.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If data shows that Reya can complete the transition without external integration, or if Pons’ graduation and retention increase with the number of tokens issued, then the judgment of migration costs and distribution quality needs to be adjusted.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Deleted documents related to ordinary asset listings, short-term price fluctuations, no incremental mechanisms, full market snapshots, and exchange withdrawal documents with expired source dates.
