Harmony proposes closing its layer 1 and migrating ONE to Ethereum

Harmony proposed shutting down its own layer-1 chain, taking a final-block snapshot of wallet balances, staked balances and exchange balances, and airdropping ERC-20 ONE on Ethereum with the same supply and issuance schedule.

The proposal remains non-binding. Smart contracts, liquidity pools, and on-chain applications cannot be automatically migrated. Users are required to exit related contracts by September 10th.
