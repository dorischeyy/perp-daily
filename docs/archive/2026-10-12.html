Papertrade tightens exposure limits after launching

Event: After allegations of oracle manipulation at 1,000x leverage platform Papertrade, the team said it was limiting exposure onchain and at the relayer. The wallet cited in reporting showed about $1.73 million in realized profits against a house pool of roughly $3.14 million. The team did not confirm allegations that addresses were banned or traders were prevented from closing positions.

Mechanism: The counterparty pool starts at zero and grows through net trader losses. When it lacks funds, profitable withdrawals queue. Reference prices, per-account exposure and pool solvency therefore interact. The new limits are a response to the incident. They do not resolve the allegations.

Measurement limitsWallet earnings and fund pool balances are based on public dashboards and media reports. These can indicate the scale of pressure, but they cannot prove manipulation, bans, or refusal to close positions.
