# Editorial review for issue 82

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Payward brought together Hyperliquid’s order book, Bitnomial clearing and NinjaTrader accounts in a licensed framework for U.S. onchain perpetuals. Binance used the same parameters to add seven U.S. equity perpetuals. Competition increasingly concerns distribution and trading infrastructure.

**What is the most important finding?**: Payward isn’t just simply integrating with a DEX. It exposes the protocols, transaction settlements, and licensed brokers’ responsibilities. This approach is closer to a practical structure than simply announcing plans for the US market.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: US onchain perpetuals have progressed from regulatory intentions and reported partnerships to identified entities with specific responsibilities. Approval is still pending. For Pons on Robinhood Chain, the leader in fees and the leader in launch count are now different.

**What decision can readers make?**: Before committing to a perpetuals distribution partnership, verify who owns the customer relationship, who handles clearing, who is eligible and what happens during disruptions. Listing count alone does not answer these questions.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If the Payward solution fails to be approved or is not actually adopted after its launch, and if there is no trading activity or open interest in Binance’s seven contracts for a long time, then Pons’ costs will decrease significantly. In such cases, the current assumptions regarding the hierarchical structure and distribution of resources need to be adjusted.

**Which news recap was removed?**: The Meta Muse application list, OpenAI’s Australian youth initiative, and another group of general listings on OKX have been deleted. Only those entries that can be updated regarding mechanisms, responsibilities, or actual distribution will be retained.

### Six reader perspectives

1. Top traders: Binance’s expansion only includes parameters; there are no depth, price differences, or open interest data. Therefore, it is not possible to determine the tradability based on these parameters. The text has separated the data with a separate line.
2. For competing products, Payward’s three-tier architecture is the most reusable. However, the list of permissions and fault responsibilities remain incomplete. Therefore, it is not appropriate to claim that the product has been fully implemented.
3. CEO strategy: The main focus of this issue is the integration of traditional financial assets and on-chain execution at the distribution layer. What matters most is who has the rights to acquire customers, settle transactions, and handle anomalies.
4. Risk control and compliance: The postponement of ONE’s termination allows the lifecycle status to be announced and withdrawn. Automatic trading cannot simply cache the final date. bStocks dividends also require distinction between uninterrupted transactions and suspended asset transfers.
5. Data analysis: The plus sign in MemeFees indicates a lower limit. The number of tokens issued covers only observable platforms. Pons’ fees over the past 30 days are used to supplement this data, but the two indicators are not combined into a single ranking.
6. General readers: Perpspad and ADD.fun have innovative mechanisms, but the project is still in its early stages. The text has removed promotional statements and clarified that there are no audited samples, no graduation, and no settlement samples.
