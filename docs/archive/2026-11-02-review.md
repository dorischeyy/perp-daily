# Perp DEX Daily editorial review · 2026-11-02

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: As stocks and other real-world assets move toward round-the-clock trading, risks increasingly arise in pricing outside market hours, dividends, custody reconciliation, collateral withdrawals, and default handling.

**What is the most important finding?**: MEXC handles dividends on equity perpetuals in three stages: reduce-only trading, a special funding payment and the resumption of trading. Chainlink, DeepBook, Reisa and RWL address pricing, custody, shared liquidity and peer-to-peer credit, respectively.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: It’s important to note that the focus has shifted from “RWA transactions can be expanded with stock tokens” to “RWA products must address company actions, continuous pricing, custody status, and exit dependencies throughout the day.”

**What decision can readers make?**: When designing or reviewing real-world asset trading and lending products, check pricing outside cash-market hours, corporate actions, order delays, idle cash, eligible assets, liquidation, and default handling.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If in actual operation, company actions can be handled without statefulness, shared pools can exit with lower latency than regular withdrawals, and peer-to-peer lending can handle defaults without taking on any risk, then it’s necessary to re-evaluate these backend dependencies.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Items related to simple expansion, price fluctuations, and no-mechanism increments have been removed. All items included are associated with verifiable products or risk assessments.
