# Editorial review

Date: 2026-09-01 · Issue 65

## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: Hyperliquid’s American path shows a specific structure for on-chain markets, licensed trading entities, clearing, and brokerage entities.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: MEXC has converted SHEIN’s pre-market perpetual futures into standard contracts. This means that RWA listing isn’t just a name change; it involves a state machine consisting of opening, index stability, order inheritance, and abnormal exits.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: RWA risk controls now consider spot market status, the last valid price and index stability together when deciding whether to allow new positions, permit only reductions or resume normal trading.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: For RWA products, the state transition occurs during market closures and abnormal deviations. Only then will it be decided whether the assets will be launched and their price sources will be configured.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If subsequent data from PAIR proves that the stock token basket can remain close to the spot price during market hours without needing to pause its issuance, then the state-based gatekeeping mechanism can be reduced. If the CFTC rejects the Payward structure, then the US-based judgment needs to be re-evaluated.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: Candidates that only report one asset, price, repurchase, or no changes in mechanisms have been removed. Each remaining candidate has a structure, scale, or clear boundaries.
