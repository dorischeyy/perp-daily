Weekly theme | HIP-3, MEXC and Gate offer parallel pre-market prices for ChangXin Memory Technologies

Event: Trade.xyz deployed a CXMT pre-market perpetual on Hyperliquid. MEXC and Gate then listed similar contracts, allowing traders without A-shares to take long or short exposure ahead of ChangXin Memory Technologies’ expected July 27 listing. This was day 37 of coverage since June 12.

Mechanism: Before listing, each venue’s order book sets the price. After listing, the index can use the renminbi share price and exchange rate. The contracts confer no shares, dividends or voting rights. Cross-border access restrictions also limit spot arbitrage, so funding payments alone cannot guarantee convergence to the A-share price.

Second-order effects: Centralized venues copied the listing within days. Differences in index transition rules, executable depth, corporate actions and geographic eligibility could matter more than the early lead. The July 27 opening would provide a public test of convergence.

ImplementationHyperliquid’s CXMT market traded about $1.32 million on its first day and had $2.41 million in open interest, well below the $50 million reference level for emerging markets.

Robinhood Wallet distributes Lighter across 120 countries while its own chain’s perpetuals remain waitlisted

Event: On day 17 of coverage since July 2, Robinhood confirmed that wallet users could directly trade gold, silver, FX and crypto perpetuals hosted by Lighter. Volume in Robinhood Chain's own perpetuals markets remained thin.

Mechanism: Wallet orders routed to Lighter generate depth and fees on that external venue. They do not automatically add to Robinhood Chain's native open interest, liquidity provider income or market data. Distribution and execution need separate measures.

Second-order effects: A large user base can reduce the cost of attracting customers. It cannot by itself supply local markets or trading depth. Aggregate DEX volume and wallet visits can obscure actual conversion to perpetuals trading when memecoin spot trades and external routing account for much of the activity.

ScaleCoinDesk put July 13 perpetuals volume on Robinhood Chain at about $5.9 million, against roughly $8.9 billion on Hyperliquid that day. The former was about 0.07% of the latter.

Aevo combines perpetuals and protective options in a one-click product

Event: On July 16, Aevo brought its full PERPS+ offering to mobile. BTC and ETH traders can choose at entry to limit maximum loss, collect a premium in return for capped upside, or set both profit and loss limits.

Mechanism: Within a single margin account, Aevo constructs, prices and executes a perpetual and an option together. The user selects a payoff range without having to choose the strike, expiry and premium separately.

Second-order effects: A simpler interface can hide the complexity of combining derivatives. The realized loss boundary still depends on options depth, execution quality on both legs and the ability to close in extreme markets. Margin and liquidation controls remain necessary.

ImplementationOnly BTC and ETH are covered. The announcement gave no usage, slippage on the options legs, or failed combination-order rate, leaving adoption and execution quality unverified.

Research finds 85% of concentrated liquidity underused, primarily when prices move to one side of the range

A weekly Dune snapshot of Uniswap, PancakeSwap and Aerodrome across seven chains found that much of the capital in concentrated liquidity pools was underused. Individual wallets accounted for 82–94% of the idle funds for which the holder could be identified.

The research found that sustained one-way price moves tended to leave positions outside their ranges for longer than high volatility that eventually reversed. Automated management can shorten idle periods at the cost of additional rebalancing, execution risk and exposure to adverse prices.

ScopeAn average of about $542 million in the sample was completely outside its active range each week. Estimated forgone fees were around $150 million annualized. Recovering those fees would itself incur costs.
