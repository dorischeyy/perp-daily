Equity perpetuals and trading costs

November 13: HRC reports that equity perpetuals account for nearly 60% of quarterly HIP-3 trading volume

HRC’s third-quarter report put equity perpetual volume at $153.8 billion, up 161% quarter over quarter and ahead of commodities at 58.8% of total HIP-3 volume. Third-party apps earned $25 million in trading interface fees, up 50% quarter over quarter. As native crypto perpetual trading rebounded, HIP-3’s share of platform-wide perpetual volume fell from 51% in July to 25% in September.

November 13: Ondo Perps introduces volume-based fee tiers across spot and perpetual markets

Ondo Perps calculates fee tiers from executed volume over the trailing 14 days and updates them daily at 00:00 UTC. Main and sub-balances share the same tier. Perpetual, tokenized equity and private-market trades count toward it. Spot and perpetuals retain distinct rates, and private markets retain their own fee schedule. Users can check their tier in Portfolio under Fee Schedule.

Trading access and user engagement

Wealthsimple integrates perpetual contracts into existing margin accounts, affecting overall account purchasing power.

After completing suitability checks and product education on mobile, eligible Wealthsimple users can trade perpetuals on crypto assets, gold and silver from an existing margin account. Gains and losses settle in U.S. dollars. These positions share buying power with stocks and futures, so losses can trigger sales of other assets in the account. The phased rollout supports market orders on mobile only.

On November 13, Cursor disclosed how a supplier key leak led to malicious extensions being distributed.

Cursor’s investigation of its October security incident linked a supplier credential exposure at Fireworks to access to historical non-privacy-mode training records and unauthorized distribution of malicious extensions.
