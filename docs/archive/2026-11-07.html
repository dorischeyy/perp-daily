November 5: CFTC staff allow clearing organizations to use trust companies for daily settlement

The CFTC’s Division of Clearing and Risk said derivatives clearing organizations could use a trust company for daily settlement with clearing members, provided they treat it as a settlement bank under existing requirements. This can connect digital asset custody with margin flows. The staff issued a no-action position, which is neither a Commission rule nor a legal conclusion.
