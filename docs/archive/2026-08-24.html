Hyperliquid’s open-interest-to-volume ratio rises to 1.82

Event: Hyperliquid open interest rose 4.0% from the previous day while 24-hour volume fell 30.6%. Its open-interest-to-volume ratio rose from 1.21 to 1.82.

Mechanism: The ratio compares outstanding positions with a day’s turnover. A rise means less trading per unit of open exposure. It says nothing by itself about the safety of new positions.

Second-order effects: If the ratio stays high, market-level exit depth and capacity to absorb liquidations in volatile conditions will matter more, even if the venue’s aggregate open interest appears stable.

Measurement limitsThe ratio covers one venue for one day. It does not measure individual asset liquidity, position direction, or user concentration.

KuCoin uses a staged mark-price transition to settle delisted contracts

Event: KuCoin plans to delist ICXUSDT and STORJUSDT perpetuals on August 26. It will stop position openings before settlement and use the final 30-minute average index price to settle both contracts.

Mechanism: At 06:30 UTC, a 180-second transition will move the mark price to the index price. Position openings stop at 06:50 and settlement follows at 07:00. The sequence gives users distinct pricing-transition, reduce-only and final-settlement stages.

Second-order effects: Staged delisting gives holders and trading bots a predictable exit window. Residual risk concentrates in index quality, liquidity near the end of trading and the settlement window.

ScopeThe notice gave no volume, open interest, or index composition for the two contracts. The evidence does not establish that liquidity alone prompted delisting.

Source material and substitution increasingly shape the copyright debate over AI training

Recent U.S. cases suggest that transformative use can support a fair-use defense for training. Obtaining training material through piracy can still constitute a separate infringement, and uses that substitute directly for the original work face a weaker defense.

ScopeThis analysis covers interim developments in several US cases. It does not establish a final national rule. Different facts or jurisdictions may produce different outcomes.
