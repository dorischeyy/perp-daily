**Which prior assessment changed?**: TradFi/RWA perpetual lines incorporate new asset hedging measures, while Robinhood Chain’s distribution lines feature enhanced fee leadership and separate supply delivery mechanisms. The advanced model evaluation lines involve adding impossible tasks and evidence of fragile environments within real systems.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating RWA trading markets, it is important to verify indicators such as market closure switches, gap pressure tests, and automatic risk controls. When restoring user funds on damaged trading platforms, it is necessary to verify that funds are truly received in batches. When deploying agents, it is essential to consider target accessibility and to monitor tools and network behavior as key criteria for successful deployment.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If Deribit’s phased contracts are not traded for a long time, and Bitget still experiences many failures after resuming operations, then Pons’ share of fees will decrease significantly once complete data is available. Additionally, Darktrace’s experiments fail to replicate proactive intrusions when artificial vulnerabilities are removed. Therefore, the current structural assessment needs to be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Announcements related to ordinary perpetual tokens, exchanges that only maintain tokens without any mechanisms or data support, and cooperation messages without any data backing have been removed. Additionally, general models that are less relevant compared to the current Agent operations and security measures have also been removed.
