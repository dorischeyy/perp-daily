## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Lighter began handing over the core of the trading system to external developers. Pons demonstrated that Robinhood Chain has observable value capture through daily fees of 5.8384 million USD. Both lines work together to address the issue of “how to monetize entrances and bases.”

**What is the most important finding?**: Pons received a 50.4% share of the fees from MemeFees. However, the fees have decreased by 49% over the past 7 days. This data sets both a threshold for scale and persistence.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: Our assessment of Robinhood Chain has changed from “wallet distribution has led to transactions” to “on-chain platforms have generated observable revenue and supply-side distribution.” However, the confidence in long-term retention has not increased.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating the foundation or distribution of transactions, it is important to consider factors such as incoming traffic, protocol revenue, supply-side distribution, and exit constraints. Do not rely solely on a single transaction or announcement to validate the product’s adoption.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If Pons’s handling fees decline rapidly back to lower levels, and there is no increase in active wallets, or if there are no new orders from external developers for Lighter’s core products, then the assumption that the distribution will evolve towards value capture and backend services is not valid.

**Which news recap was removed?**: The Aster batch adjustment funding rate cycle, Variational weekly integration, Stonkfun regular token issuance, and OpenAI Habitat have been removed from the list.

### Six reader perspectives

1. For top-tier traders/big whales: Lighter only discloses changes in functionality, without providing details on transaction depth or completion times for withdrawals. SideSwap has resumed trading, but the peg-out mechanism has not been restored. It’s important not to misinterpret “tradingable” as “able to exit”.
2. Product managers of competing products: Lighter shares the core of its trading system, while Entropy focuses on building tools. Both companies are competing for market share. However, there are no official agreements regarding customer attribution, risk management permissions, or liability in case of failures. Therefore, it’s not possible to replicate their systems directly.
3. CEO/Strategy: The main focus this time is to distribute entry points, establish a trading foundation, and start capturing value in a hierarchical manner. If only one decision can be made, it’s important to establish a list of revenue sources and responsibilities for cooperation.
4. Skeptics: Pons’ 50.4% rate is only valid within the MemeFees sample. The cumulative repurchase amount on Launchpad.meme is only $7,139.08. Neither statistic proves long-term adoption.
5. Editor-in-Chief: Lighter and Pons formed the main line of operations. Routine coin issuance, point distribution, and repetitive AI scripts have been removed. BIBO has been clearly downgraded to the main mechanism for this week, to avoid impersonating current news stories.
6. For new users: explanations for builder fees, peg-out, and ve(3,3) mechanisms are provided. Each large number indicates a time window, scope, or data that is still missing.
