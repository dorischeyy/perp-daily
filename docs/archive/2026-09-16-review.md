# Editorial review for issue 79

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: edgeX has expanded its foreign exchange perpetual contracts to all-day trading. TradeXYZ has established its own scale. Meanwhile, the costs associated with supply and the standards for accounts reveal the costs involved in scaling up the platform.

**What is the most important finding?**: The open interest for tradeXYZ on a daily basis has reached 38% of the Hyperliquid level. However, not all of the total assets on the platform can be classified as RWA. This indicates that it is necessary to further break down the asset categories, including the open interest, holding periods, and cost contributions.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The traditional opening time in the FX market can be continued. However, for full-day trading, it is necessary to verify the weekend price formation, depth, risk limits, and abnormal recoveries separately.

**What decision can readers make?**: Before offering a new FX market around the clock, verify weekend feeds, mark-price deviations, open-interest caps, triggers for reduce-only mode and how the basis converges when the underlying market reopens.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If edgeX’s weekend performance remains at or below the same level as weekdays, and there is no increase in abnormal prices, or if tradeXYZ’s data shows a continuous decline in RWA OI, then the assessment of the full-day FX and RWA scales for this period needs to be adjusted.

**Which news recap was removed?**: The Bitget ordinary listing, expired WTI perpetual contracts, borrowing parameters from seven days ago, future date forecasts, and pure price fluctuation drafts have been deleted.

### Six reader perspectives

1. Top traders/big whales: edgeX has not disclosed information about weekend market depth, mark price deviations, or abnormal price handling. Therefore, it cannot be assumed that 24/7 trading capabilities can handle large positions at any time.
2. Product manager of competing products: tradeXYZ has established itself as a significant player, but the snapshot does not indicate how much of its revenue comes from RWA, nor does it prove the sustainability of its positions.
3. CEO/Strategy: Parabola and edgeX are both launched with Arc. This indicates that the new chain can provide centralized supply for initial launches. However, there is no evidence of cross-product retention or revenue generation.
4. Skeptics: The share of MemeFees is affected by the denominator of underreporting. The graduation volume only covers the platform it connects to. Neither of the two lists can be generalized to the entire market.
5. Editor-in-Chief: The four columns cover trading mechanisms, issuance migration, regulatory and wallet infrastructure, and model productization. There are no records of standard coin listings or price sheets.
6. Newcomers: Each entry includes details about events, mechanisms, and boundaries. Charges, procedural votes, installation quantities, and official availability status have not been mixed together to form a final conclusion.
