New trading products

October 20: Kraken Desktop adds synchronized order placement across multiple prop accounts

Kraken Desktop now lets prop traders place orders across their own evaluation and funded accounts. Follower accounts can mirror the lead account’s order quantity or size orders in proportion to equity. Stops, take-profits, edits and cancellations sync as well. A follower that fails its rules is skipped. A violation on the lead account stops copying. The feature cannot follow other traders.
