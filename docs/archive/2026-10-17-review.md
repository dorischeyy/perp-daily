# Editorial review for issue 106

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Binance has tightened contract risks, directly affecting existing positions and grids. The three major Japanese banks have taken steps to facilitate the issuance of joint stablecoins across borders. OpenAI has integrated multi-agent parallelism and collaborative delivery into the same product.

**What is the most important finding?**: Japan’s three major banks are not only involved in pilot projects for stablecoins, but also in verifying the issuance, transfer, redemption, and AML/CFT-related responsibilities through joint trusts and cross-border scenarios.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The trading backend services have evolved from being accessed by brokers to being used for multi-bank joint issuance. The advanced model evaluation line now includes detailed identity and authorization details for each transaction. Production governance no longer relies solely on platform-specific permissions.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating changes in perpetual risk, it is necessary to first verify the impact of old strategies and automatic policies. When evaluating on-chain settlements and agent transactions, it is necessary to verify the responsible parties, individual authorizations, revocation capabilities, and production adoption.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If the bank joint scheme does not lead to real transactions, if x401 is not adopted across sites, if ChatGPT’s collaboration features cannot be used in the target workspace in the long term, or if Binance’s adjustments do not result in observable risk differences, then the structural judgment needs to be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: The candidate GPT-Live voice updates from NVIDIA and AtScale that cannot be fully read from the source page, as well as other weaker updates from the same day, have been deleted. Additionally, there are no details regarding normal asset listing and price sheets.

3. CEO and Strategy: The simultaneous emergence of multi-bank joint issuance and community bank core system integration indicates that the competition for blockchain-based currencies is shifting towards existing networks. Priorities should be focused on role division and operational evidence rather than the names of the blockchain networks.
4. Skeptics: The cases involving MemeToro and Treasure were reported by the manufacturers themselves. 1WIN promised to refund any refunds. Marqeta and Jack Henry are both planned or integrated announcements. The text must maintain its original boundaries.
5. Editor-in-Chief: The most significant changes are seen in Binance, Japanese banks, and OpenAI. Launchpad only retains points that can be verified, distributed proportionally, and has a structured supply system. There are no arbitrary financing arrangements.
6. New reader: The perpetuals section separates the event from the mechanism. Other sections identify who is responsible and how the process works. Context adds only an adoption gap, a measurement limit or a scale comparison.
