Aster adjusts perpetual taker fees by market group

Event: Effective September 7, Aster adjusted taker fees on RWA and Group B perpetuals. VIP maker fees remained zero, and the change did not affect other standard markets.

Mechanism: The platform no longer charges the same rate for all perpetual contracts. Instead, it charges separately based on asset and market categories. At the same time, incentives for resting orders are still in place.

Second-order effects: Competition shifts from unified low rates to market-cost-based segmentation. Traders need to consider target categories, account levels, and maker/taker roles when comparing venues.

Measurement limitsPublic updates confirm the timing of fee adjustments. However, complete new rates for each level are not provided, making it impossible to calculate individual cost changes.

Cronos rolls back nearly two hours of chain history to recover most losses

Cronos reported that attackers manipulated TONIC collateral and lent out 120.4 million USD. Verifiers then rolled back 10,961 blocks, recovering approximately 111.2 million USD.

There was still 9.19 million USD transferred before the chain was shut down, and it was not recovered. All transactions that occurred within the 1 hour and 54 minutes window were canceled, regardless of their relevance. The funds were restored at the expense of finality.

ScopeApproximately 92.4% of the affected value has been rolled back. The remaining 7.6% has been removed from the chain. Restoring balances does not mean eliminating the costs associated with cross-platform reconciliation.

Google and Cathay Pacific expand their AI contrail avoidance trial

Google and Cathay Pacific extended AI-based anti-stellar drift technology to long-haul routes in the Asia-Pacific region. The system combines satellite images and weather forecasts to suggest slight adjustments in altitude before takeoff and during flight.
