## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: GRVT has explicitly linked the funding payments on perpetual futures to traditional CFD hedging costs for the first time. The trading hours have been brought back to the traditional market opening hours, thereby directly affecting the cost of positions and risk assessment during market closures.
**What is the most important finding?**: GRVT's seven commodity contracts charge funding on business days and accrue three days at once before the weekend. Liquidation remains possible while the underlying market is closed. These rules show how real-world asset perpetuals are evolving into hedging tools with different mechanics from round-the-clock crypto contracts.
**Which prior assessment changed?**: We expanded our assessment beyond equity perpetual listings and volume to examine the source of funding rates, trading sessions, and liquidation outside underlying market hours. This raised our confidence that venues are differentiating their product rules.
**What decision can readers make?**: Before deciding to launch a commodity or pre-listing perpetual, verify who quotes the funding rate, how the contract behaves outside underlying market hours, how positions migrate, and how trading bots resume.
**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: Reduce confidence in this assessment of product differences if GRVT’s actual funding payments consistently diverge from traditional hedging costs or if liquidations during closures depart from the guide.
**Which news recap was removed?**: The mark price adjustments for KuCoin’s non-standard contracts have been deleted. The trade.xyz page only has a mirror page. The claims regarding predict.fun’s distribution have not been confirmed by the official authorities.
