Weekly theme | Binance adds USD1 settlement to its SpaceX perpetual range

Event: Binance launched SPCXUSD1 at 09:00 UTC on July 20 with up to 25× leverage and around-the-clock trading. It was Binance’s first equity perpetual quoted and settled directly in USD1. This was day 13 of coverage since July 9.

Mechanism: USD1 had first served as supplementary collateral and later became a settlement unit for BTC and ETH. With SPCXUSD1, traders can now choose USDT- or USD1-denominated contracts on the same reference asset. Each has its own margin, funding and P&L.

Second-order effects: A high-profile stock contract can distribute a stablecoin as well as attract order flow. Without shared depth, the two contracts could also split liquidity and open interest.

ImplementationThe contract and its rules were live. First-day open interest, order-book depth and the distribution of USD1 balances had not been disclosed.

Weekly theme | CME reports $1 billion in derivatives volume across its first six weekends of 24/7 trading

Event: CME's July 14 quarterly report said crypto futures and options traded $1 billion over their first six weekends after switching to 24/7 trading on May 29. Q2 averaged 250,800 contracts traded per day and 216,000 contracts of daily open interest.

Mechanism: The regulated venue offers continuous trading through central clearing, broker margin arrangements and scheduled weekend maintenance. Customers hold dated futures or options, which do not require perpetual funding payments to maintain exposure.

Second-order effects: As regulated venues adopt 24/7 trading, perpetuals DEXs increasingly compete on self-custody, listing flexibility, margin efficiency and depth in smaller markets.

Measurement limitsThe $1 billion covers only the first six weekends and combines futures with options. It shows actual use, but is not directly comparable to a perpetuals DEX's daily notional volume.
