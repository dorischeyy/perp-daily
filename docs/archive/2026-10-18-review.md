# Editorial review for issue 107

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: OKX introduced three layers of funds to cover deficits from onchain perpetuals liquidations. SBI and Startale advanced a cross-issuer stablecoin interoperability pilot. Microsoft introduced independent organizational identities, persistent memory and event triggers for agents.

**What is the most important finding?**: Microsoft’s persistent agents are no longer just temporary chat tools. They become long-term execution entities within organizations, with accounts, credentials, memories, and triggers. The evaluation boundaries thus extend from model outputs to identities, permissions, and startup conditions.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: Robinhood Chain has added pre-sale launch channels, but SEED’s early liquidity and concentration of holders remain weak. The advanced model evaluation line includes new samples of persistent Agents at the organizational level.

**What decision can readers make?**: Assess perpetuals safeguards by distinguishing backstop takeover, rebalancing, replenishment and auto-deleveraging. For agent launches, examine account ownership, credential scope, session revocation and activation conditions separately.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If OKX’s three-tier funding pools do not actually cover margin trading, stablecoin interoperability remains a long-term issue within the alliance, Microsoft’s capabilities do not lead to practical applications, or RobinSeed fails to maintain continuous liquidity, then the structural judgment needs to be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Projects like AtScale, Aligned, and others with extremely low TVL have been removed from the official list. They did not use traditional listing methods, financing, or price sheets to increase their numbers.

### Six reader perspectives

1. Top traders: The architecture of OKX’s funding pool and the migration rules from Bybit can affect tail risk and old warehouse management. However, PerpFinder is merely a order book, and it cannot be used to determine the best execution location throughout the day.
2. Product managers of competing products: RobinSeed and yore.fun have expanded their pre-sale and fair launch channels. The real differences lie in graduation rates, retention, liquidity, and creator revenue, rather than just the launch slogan.
3. CEO and strategy: Stablecoin competition has evolved from single-currency issuance to interoperability and banking core systems. Priorities should be given to issuer responsibilities, fiat connection, and adoption, rather than blockchain names.
4. Skeptics: The data from RobinSeed, Tasklet, and HireFoundry comes from early pages or self-reported data by the manufacturers. The text retains details about concentration, coverage, and gaps in customer adoption.
5. The most significant changes were made in OKX, SBI, and Microsoft. All 12 items involved mechanisms or responsibility breakdown. No ordinary activities were given more importance than structural updates.
6. For new users, Perp strictly uses two-stage approaches for events and mechanisms. Other sections allow a maximum of two paragraphs per item. Context is only used to describe scale, implementation, or boundaries of information.
