UK crypto authorization application window opens

Event: The UK FCA opened applications under its new cryptoasset authorization regime at 07:00 on September 30. The window runs until February 28, 2027 and covers activities including stablecoin issuance, trading venues, arranging transactions, custody and staking.

Mechanism: Existing enterprises that have applied for but not yet completed approval can continue operating as long as they meet the conditions. Those that do not apply on time can only fulfill their old contracts within the transition period. They cannot sign new contracts with existing or new customers. The complete system will come into effect on October 25, 2027.

ScopeExisting anti-money laundering regulations, payment or electronic currency registrations do not automatically translate into new licenses. Whether services can continue depends on the timing of the application and the activities involved in the business.
