## A0 Reader value

1. **Why open this edition today?**: Across four major perpetuals venues, volume fell 22.4% while open interest rose 1.6%. Hyperliquid's open-interest-to-volume ratio rose to 1.82. KuCoin also documented the successive states of a contract delisting.
2. **What is the most important finding?**: Before ceasing trading, KuCoin completed the transition from the mark price to the index price. This indicates that delisting is not a simple button press, but rather a path involving the migration of prices, orders, and settlements.
3. **Which prior assessment changed?**: Market delisting is now treated as a distinct lifecycle. The rules should specify when new positions stop, when reduce-only trading begins, how the pricing reference changes and how final settlement works.
4. **What decision can readers make?**: When designing market exit rules, the product team can first define user notifications, unexecuted orders, Keeper, oracle systems, settlement prices, and the behavior of integration partners in various states. Then, they can determine the specific asset parameters.
5. **What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If subsequent snapshots show that the transaction process is quick to recover, and the OI decreases, then the slowdown in turnover is merely noise for a single day. If KuCoin does not have any real constraints during the delisting process, or if it still relies on manual exceptions, then the scalability of the phased mechanism will also decrease.
6. **Which news recap was removed?**: News about Upbit’s surge in daily trading volume, speculation regarding the board seat of a16z, and financing news related to startups before August 20 have been deleted. These stories do not provide any verifiable new mechanisms or changes in decision-making.
