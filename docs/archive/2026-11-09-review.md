# Editorial review

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Bybit has added three new 24-hour trading options for US stocks. At the same time, it has reduced the single-lot capacity for 58 USDT perpetual contracts starting from November 9th. Product expansion and risk limits are being adjusted simultaneously.

**What is the most important finding?**: Priority should be given to Bybit, as the same platform completed both asset expansion and order limit reduction within three days, which directly affects trading capacity. The boundary is that announcements do not disclose adjustments that trigger actual changes in depth.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: TradFi perpetual contracts are no longer just about increasing the number of targets and trading hours. The platform is beginning to actively tighten the maximum risk capacity after expanding the list of available orders. The growth of supply and the depth of capacity need to be verified separately.

**What decision can readers make?**: Product and risk management teams could separate the items such as trading hours, maximum orders, pending orders during market closures, automatic liquidation, and settlement prices into separate checks. Instead of using the term “launched” to verify executability, more detailed checks would be necessary.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If Bybit restores its original limits and discloses true depth stability, or if data shows that limit changes are due to mechanical parameter migration rather than risk control, then the judgment of simultaneous adjustment of expansion and capacity should be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Ordinary coin listings and single-model access candidates that lack settlement details have been removed. Only those changes that affect transaction execution, exit procedures, or Agent operation boundaries will be retained.
