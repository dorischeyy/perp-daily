# Perp DEX Daily editorial review · 2026-10-27

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: Perpetuals platforms are bringing traditional assets, premarket pricing and margin subsidies into the same account. Readers should distinguish additional listings, price discovery and the potential return on funds left on the platform.

**What is the most important finding?**: PREDICT’s perpetual contracts rely on proprietary pricing from the exchange before the formation of the stablecoin index. The mark price has a maximum limit, but it cannot be considered a fair price for spot transactions.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The comparison of traditional-asset perpetuals now considers premarket pricing, conditions for earning yield on collateral and limits on withdrawing funds alongside the number of assets listed.

**What decision can readers make?**: In product evaluation, it is first necessary to verify the source of the index, the conditions for subsidies, and the actual retention of data. Only after that can decisions be made regarding expansion, integration into pre-market trading, or increasing margin gains.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If transactions and users remain stable after the event ends, the pre-market mark price will continue to closely follow mature spot indices. Additionally, subsidies will be converted into stable underlying returns. Today’s cautious judgment requires an upward adjustment.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Announcements that only include new names, without any changes in pricing or funding rules have been removed. Also, old updates from seven AI companies that don’t involve any new facts have been deleted.
