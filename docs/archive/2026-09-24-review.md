# Editorial review for issue 86

## A0 Reader value

**Why open this edition today?**: BitMEX completed its shutdown, and Binance incorporated tokenized stocks into its collateral management system. The CFTC classified similar contracts as high-risk types, making market exits, collateralization, and access boundaries more specific on the same day.

**What is the most important finding?**: Tokenized securities are no longer just used as trading assets. Binance uses bStocks as a combination of margin assets. Blockchain.com and NYSE are exploring the use of connected wallets for distribution and regulated ATS. Galaxy enables interest-bearing stablecoins to serve as collateral for institutions. The availability of these assets is extending to both collateral and settlement layers.

**Which prior assessment changed?**: The rules for prediction markets have evolved from a broad self-authentication mechanism in July to a system that imposes restrictions on certain contract types and conducts anti-manipulation analyses for each contract. The TradFi/RWA lines have also expanded from listing and matching engines to a portfolio collateral system.

**What decision can readers make?**: When evaluating new assets and contract events, it is necessary to verify whether transactions are actually open, whether assets can be borrowed or only used as collateral, the index and discount rate, whether settlements can be independently verified, and whether regulatory documents are advisory or final rules.

**What evidence would overturn the conclusion?**: If bStocks as collateral are not used in the long term, if regulatory approval cannot be obtained through NYSE cooperation, if sUSDS remains only in Galaxy’s own funds allocation, or if the CFTC does not include contract-specific analysis in its official rules, then the judgment regarding the advancement of the collateral layer and access layer needs to be downgraded.

**Which news recap was removed?**: Deleted information includes details on expanding regular contracts, financing messages without product parameters, progress updates from SoFi and Pyth, as well as market entries with only price fluctuations without any changes in mechanisms.
