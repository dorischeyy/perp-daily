November 11: Bybit lists Dangote pre-IPO perpetuals with an adjustment planned once actual shares outstanding are disclosed

Bybit listed the DANGOTEUSDT premarket perpetual with up to 5x leverage and a fixed 0.005% funding rate every four hours during premarket trading. Initial pricing uses estimated shares outstanding.
